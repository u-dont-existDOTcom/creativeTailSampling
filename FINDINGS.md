## Current survivors

### C001 — Active normative edge-case search
**Narrow survivor after retrieval-ensemble attack.** Reflective equilibrium, diagnostic thought experiments, legal case comparison, independent pre-deliberative elicitation, deliberative polling/Delphi, constitutional-design experiments, precedent, and democratic experimentalism already contain the component mechanisms.

The retained residual is the **integrated pipeline**:

> Select concrete cases specifically for their ability to discriminate plausible constitutional/value interpretations; collect independent answers before deliberation; then preserve resolved cases as a durable regression-test suite for future interpretations.

Do not claim novelty for case-based moral reasoning, thought experiments, independent elicitation, deliberation sequencing, or precedent individually.

### C003 — Asset-gated cultural mutation
**Narrow provisional survivor.** Branch inheritance can pre-filter which institutional variants receive enough land/capital/reputation to become viable competitors, making apparent canonical superiority partly a pre-selection capitalization effect.

### C005 — Reproductive-variance / superstar-reproduction trap
**Narrow survivor after retrieval-ensemble attack; empirically strengthened.** Parent→daughter fission/reproduction is already explicit in Hutterite colony demography, Amish church-district fission, organizational genealogy, church multiplication, and social-innovation scaling. The survivor is therefore **not** `treat communal spread as reproduction`.

The retained residual is the evaluative rule:

> Judge communal reproducibility by the **distribution of daughter units per parent community**, not total descendants or mean daughters alone. Track zero-daughter probability, median daughters, dispersion/variance, concentration in prolific parents, generation interval, granddaughter reproduction, and subsidy/propagule structure.

Mature-parent Hutterite signatures:
- Manitoba Schmiedeleut: `P(K=0)=4.08%`, median `K=2`, top-10% share ~22.3%.
- Lehrerleut 1918–1953: `P(K=0)=3.45%`, median `K=1`, top-10% share 24.53%.
- Dariusleut 1918–1953: `P(K=0)=0%`, median `K=2`, top-10% share 20.0%.

Operational shorthand: **make reproduction boring**.

### C006 — Descendant inflation / lineage-size sampling bias
**Narrow research-control transfer after retrieval-ensemble attack.** Lineage heterogeneity, parent/progeny organizational genealogy, founding/population units, commune sampling-frame choices, and related dependence problems are established.

The retained residual is the explicit communal-replication estimand control:

> State whether the estimand/weighting unit is a random founding attempt/root, lineage, extant community, current resident, or future movement contribution; treat an extant-community sample as potentially **descendant-size-biased** rather than silently equating it with a sample of independent founding attempts.

### C009 — Cohort-composition admission
**Provisional; market-thickness bounded.** Applicant value can be non-separable. Use set-level admission analysis only when several plausible applicants/openings genuinely overlap—especially founding, daughter formation, new sites, and expansion waves.

### C011 — Shadow governance
**Provisional transfer.** Before transferring authority to a substantially new governance process, run it nonbinding in parallel on the same admissible cases and analyze divergence before live adoption.

### C012 — Standing constitutional-relation testing
**Provisional, narrowed.** Maintain versioned tests for relations the constitution claims should hold—symmetry, irrelevance/invariance, monotonicity, jurisdiction invariance, etc.—not merely individual example answers.

### C013 — Federated reciprocal applicant clearing after exploration
**Narrow provisional transfer.** After visits/trials reveal reciprocal preferences, coordinate commitments movement-wide so compatible seeker–vacancy pairs are not lost to isolated timing/search decisions, preserving opt-out and local admission authority.

### C015 — Governance rights-liveness verification
**Narrow formal-methods residual after retrieval-ensemble attack.** Administrative-law and governance literature already documents legally granted authority becoming unusable through quorum loss/vacancies, ex-ante continuity mechanisms, delegation, recusal, procedural dead states, and explicit safety/liveness tradeoffs in voting/governance.

The retained residual is:

> Apply a **unified adversarial reachable-state verification procedure** to the whole governance-rights architecture, testing safety, liveness, and bounded liveness across interacting recusal, vacancy, quorum, jurisdiction, deadline, delegation, noncooperation, capacity, and conflicting-valid-path states.

Do not claim novelty for the proposition that a right can exist in text yet fail in practice.

### C017 — Intervention-aware rescue forecasts
**Provisional transfer.** Do not use an intervention-produced outcome as an unadjusted label for the forecast that triggered the intervention. Define the intervention regime, freeze pre-rescue evidence, separate forecast quality from rescue efficacy, and record rescue/subsidy exposure.

### C018 — Replicated-governance tombstones
**Provisional transfer.** When a high-consequence governance pattern is retired for a known failure, propagate a semantic deprecation record through the same daughter/template/fork channels that could otherwise resurrect it.

### C019 — Power-targeted federation voting design
**Provisional voting-power transfer.** Design/audit the **power distribution intended**, not the visible weight vector. Model local and federation tiers, weights + quota, and actual pivotal power; stress behavioral assumptions and re-audit after structural change. Do **not** reduce this to `use square-root weights`.

Reusable tool: `analysis/weighted_voting_power.py`.

### C020 — Fission/merge representation-effect audit
**Provisional transfer linking voting power to reproduction.** Daughter formation/merger can create an accidental political subsidy or penalty. Calculate before/after power and use an aligned-preference split/merge counterfactual to isolate influence created solely by changed organizational boundaries.

### C021 — Scrutiny-conditioned evidence accounting
**Provisional transfer from surveillance/selective-label research; passed internal corpus audit.** When suspicion changes observation intensity, later evidence must preserve that observation regime. Monitoring-discovered incident counts cannot silently become independent confirmation of the suspicion that caused extra scrutiny.

### C022 — Cross-community measurement canaries
**Provisional measurement transfer; passed internal corpus audit.** Before pooling locally interpreted outcomes, circulate identical synthetic cases and compare how communities/research teams classify them. Use disagreement to detect threshold/schema heterogeneity; validate vignette equivalence; preserve raw versus calibrated layers.

C022 differs from C001: C001 probes **normative disagreement**; C022 probes **empirical classification comparability**.

### C023 — Cumulative disclosure-budget / differencing guard
**Provisional privacy transfer; passed internal corpus audit.** Small-community research privacy must be audited across the **joint history of overlapping aggregate releases**, not one table at a time. Maintain a release ledger, test differencing/reconstruction routes, use pooled/access-tiered publication, and treat formal privacy composition as a longitudinal constraint.

### C024 — Non-custodial federation research
**Provisional privacy/trust transfer; passed internal corpus audit.** For suitable sensitive aggregate questions, minimize not only who may read raw responses but whether readable centralized raw responses ever need to exist.

Use secure aggregation/MPC/federated analytics or split-trust computation where justified so neither local leadership nor the federation research office sees plaintext member inputs. Pair this with **C023 output-disclosure control**: hiding inputs does not make a tiny exact aggregate safe to release.

Full audit: `runs/2026-08-15-orthogonal-tail-batch-53.md`.

### C025 — Community resolution / living-will architecture
**Narrow survivor after retrieval-ensemble attack.** FEMA/nonprofit continuity and devolution already preserve essential functions during disruption and can transfer functions to another organization; nursing-facility closure practice already turns resident care into a transfer duty; Community Land Trust dissolution rules can preserve housing/land stewardship beyond the original corporation; NIST/CISA already map multi-function community resilience.

The broad claims `preserve essential functions if an organization fails` and `housing stewardship can outlive the legal entity` are therefore not C025.

The retained residual is:

> Predefine an integrated **permanent communal dissolution** protocol that assigns bridge/successor responsibility across the complete member-critical bundle—housing transition, food/water, medication/care, childcare/education, cash/benefits, transport, records, family contact, and animal/land obligations—without making recovery or reconstitution of the commune itself a prerequisite for continuity.

Core rule:

> **Design failure so the people and critical functions do not have to fail with the institution.**

Full prior audit: `runs/2026-08-15-orthogonal-tail-batch-56.md`; retrieval-ensemble narrowing: `runs/2026-08-16-retrieval-ensemble-round-001-final.md`.

### C026 — Adulthood option grant / neutral transition package
**NARROW provisional survivor after internal corpus collision audit.** The communities corpus already owns **non-inherited adult membership**, child exit capacity, usable-exit concerns, transition support, and leaving-fund evidence. Therefore those are not C026.

The only retained residual is:

> **Portable launch/transition resources that make adult non-membership viable should be available before a person raised in the community opts into the adult property/work/governance regime, rather than requiring them to join first and later qualify for exit support.**

This is a timing/symmetry claim about meaningful consent, not a claim to proportional communal property ownership.

Full audit: `runs/2026-08-15-orthogonal-tail-batch-59.md` and `runs/2026-08-15-project-corpus-collision-audit-c021-c026.md`.

## Recent demotions after external retrieval

- **C016 — Verifiably unpredictable federation audit sampling:** **REJECTED from the strict originality ledger.** RFC 3797 already specifies freezing/publishing the eligible pool, precommitting the exact selection algorithm, using future public randomness outside administrator control, keeping the pool immutable, and making the draw publicly replayable. Public-randomness-beacon practice also applies essentially the same architecture explicitly to audit sampling. Preserve C016 only as a practical design rule.

## Recent practical-only results

- **C016 practical rule:** for independently justified random audits, publish scope/rights, freeze the eligible universe before future randomness is knowable, precommit deterministic selection, use uncontrollable future randomness, and make the realized draw replayable afterward.
- **B48:** Hutterite destination-blind fission is target-known but high-value: construct viable groups first, assign mother/daughter destination later under a precommitted neutral process.
- **B49:** process genealogy is much sparser than parent→daughter genealogy; create prospective branching records and preserve `unknown` instead of imputing branch custom.
- **B50:** rotate authority without resetting competence; audit durable trainers/records custodians/advisers for shadow power.
- **B51:** the research center's selected `best practice` suffers winner's curse; preserve discovery/confirmation separation and selection-aware inference.
- **B52:** admission requirements are selection pressures; audit information gained per applicant burden and who disappears from the funnel.
- **B54:** minimum viable community scale and minimum viable federation social/demographic scale are different; intercommunity youth mobility can be part of generational infrastructure.
- **B55:** portable safeguarding data should be structured, scoped, versioned evidence/risk packets rather than federation-wide person labels.
- **B57:** in tiny communes, expressive responses can be identifiable from style/context even when names are removed; do not promise fake anonymity.
- **B58:** privacy starts before submission; shoulder-surfing, unattended screens/paper, shared devices, and completion timing require a separate collection-scene privacy layer.

## Method findings

- **M001:** formalization is not novelty.
- **M002:** user familiarity veto dominates model confidence.
- **M003:** external nearest-neighbor attack is mandatory before promotion; after Round 001 this means **mandatory routine Exa semantic search plus mandatory deep survivor escalation**, not one generic web search.
- **M004:** nonstandard structural cross-domain transfer can count; same-domain rediscovery cannot.
- **M005:** empty batches are successful.
- **M006:** terminology changes do not rescue an existing structure.
- **M007:** mathematically surprising but materially irrelevant transfers stay demoted.
- **M008:** originality and practical usefulness require separate dispositions.
- **M009:** known source mechanisms survive only when the target transfer changes an actual design/test/measurement decision.
- **M010:** when a broad analogy collides, narrow until a distinct operational object remains or the branch dies.
- **M011:** accountability and adversarial unpredictability can coexist through commit-before-randomness + replay-after-selection.
- **M012:** intervention-triggering predictions require intervention-aware evaluation.
- **M013:** negative institutional knowledge must propagate through replication channels, not merely archives.
- **M014:** weighted federations must design power under the complete rule, not visible weights.
- **M015:** reproduction can carry an implicit federation political subsidy/penalty.
- **M016:** when governance attention changes observability, observation regime is part of evidence provenance.
- **M017:** empirical comparison needs a measurement-comparability gate before pooling.
- **M018:** small-N confidentiality is a property of the whole release history and adversary knowledge, not each release independently.
- **M019:** privacy architecture needs separate input-confidentiality and output-disclosure layers; solving one does not solve the other.
- **M020:** in tiny co-residential groups, privacy also has independent participation, collection-scene, and response-fingerprint layers; protecting storage alone is insufficient.
- **M021:** for an active research project, the project's own authoritative synthesis/crosswalk/ledger/lessons must be checked **before external novelty search**; internal rediscovery is an immediate demotion or narrowing event.
- **M022:** adding a second shallow retrieval provider does not automatically improve novelty recall. Measure **unique correct collision yield** and incremental recall rather than counting additional URLs.
- **M023:** deep multi-source research can materially improve collision detection even when the same provider's shallow search performs poorly; shallow and deep retrieval are distinct stages and must be benchmarked separately.
- **M024:** retrieval should be asymmetric: keep retrieval out of generation to preserve tail creativity, then maximize precedent recall aggressively before promotion.
