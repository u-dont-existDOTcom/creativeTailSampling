## Current survivors

### C001 — Active normative edge-case search
**Provisional transfer.** Use high-information concrete cases to distinguish plausible constitutional/value interpretations; collect independent answers before deliberation and preserve resolved cases as regression tests.

### C003 — Asset-gated cultural mutation
**Narrow provisional survivor.** Branch inheritance can pre-filter which institutional variants receive enough land/capital/reputation to become viable competitors, making apparent canonical superiority partly a pre-selection capitalization effect.

### C005 — Reproductive-variance / superstar-reproduction trap
**Empirically strengthened.** Reproducibility requires the parent-offspring distribution, not total spread or mean daughters alone. Track `P(K=0)`, median, variance/mean, top-decile share, generation interval, granddaughter reproduction, and subsidy/propagule structure.

Mature-parent Hutterite signatures:
- Manitoba Schmiedeleut: `P(K=0)=4.08%`, median `K=2`, top-10% share ~22.3%.
- Lehrerleut 1918–1953: `P(K=0)=3.45%`, median `K=1`, top-10% share 24.53%.
- Dariusleut 1918–1953: `P(K=0)=0%`, median `K=2`, top-10% share 20.0%.

Operational shorthand: **make reproduction boring**.

### C006 — Descendant inflation / lineage-size sampling bias
**Research control.** Prolific lineages mechanically contribute more extant observations. State whether the estimand is a random founding attempt/root, lineage, extant community, current resident, or future movement contribution.

### C009 — Cohort-composition admission
**Provisional; market-thickness bounded.** Applicant value can be non-separable. Use set-level admission analysis only when several plausible applicants/openings genuinely overlap—especially founding, daughter formation, new sites, and expansion waves.

### C011 — Shadow governance
**Provisional transfer.** Before transferring authority to a substantially new governance process, run it nonbinding in parallel on the same admissible cases and analyze divergence before live adoption.

### C012 — Standing constitutional-relation testing
**Provisional, narrowed.** Maintain versioned tests for relations the constitution claims should hold—symmetry, irrelevance/invariance, monotonicity, jurisdiction invariance, etc.—not merely individual example answers.

### C013 — Federated reciprocal applicant clearing after exploration
**Narrow provisional transfer.** After visits/trials reveal reciprocal preferences, coordinate commitments movement-wide so compatible seeker–vacancy pairs are not lost to isolated timing/search decisions, preserving opt-out and local admission authority.

### C015 — Governance rights-liveness verification
**Provisional formal-methods transfer.** A right can exist in text yet be dead in a reachable state. Verify safety, liveness, bounded liveness, and practical quorum-safety under realistic recusal, vacancy, jurisdiction, deadline, delegation, noncooperation, capacity, and conflicting-valid-path states.

### C016 — Verifiably unpredictable federation audit sampling
**Provisional transfer.** When random auditing is independently justified: publish scope/rights; freeze the eligible universe before future randomness is knowable; precommit deterministic selection; use uncontrollable future randomness; make the realized draw replayable afterward.

### C017 — Intervention-aware rescue forecasts
**Provisional transfer.** Do not use an intervention-produced outcome as an unadjusted label for the forecast that triggered the intervention. Define the intervention regime, freeze pre-rescue evidence, separate forecast quality from rescue efficacy, and record rescue/subsidy exposure.

Full audit: `runs/2026-08-15-orthogonal-tail-batch-23.md`.

### C018 — Replicated-governance tombstones
**Provisional transfer.** When a high-consequence governance pattern is retired for a known failure, propagate a semantic deprecation record through the same daughter/template/fork channels that could otherwise resurrect it.

Full audit: `runs/2026-08-15-orthogonal-tail-batch-25.md`.

### C019 — Power-targeted federation voting design
**Provisional voting-power transfer.** Design/audit the **power distribution intended**, not the visible weight vector. Define representation objective; model local and federation tiers; compute pivotal power under weights + quota; stress bloc/correlation/delegate behavior; solve weights and quota jointly toward the target.

Do **not** reduce this to `use square-root weights`.

Reusable tool: `analysis/weighted_voting_power.py`.

### C020 — Fission/merge representation-effect audit
**Provisional transfer linking voting power to reproduction.** Daughter formation/merger can create an accidental political subsidy or penalty. Calculate before/after power and use an aligned-preference split/merge counterfactual to isolate how much influence changes solely because organizational boundaries changed.

### C021 — Scrutiny-conditioned evidence accounting
**Provisional transfer from surveillance/selective-label research.** When suspicion changes how intensely a person is observed, later evidence must preserve the observation regime. Monitoring-discovered incident counts cannot silently become independent confirmation of the suspicion that caused extra scrutiny.

### C022 — Cross-community measurement canaries
**Provisional measurement transfer.** Before pooling locally interpreted outcomes, periodically circulate identical synthetic cases and measure how communities/research teams classify them. Use disagreement to detect threshold/schema heterogeneity; validate vignette equivalence; preserve raw vs calibrated layers.

C022 differs from C001: C001 probes hidden **normative disagreement**; C022 probes **empirical classification comparability**.

Full audit: `runs/2026-08-15-orthogonal-tail-batch-38.md`.

### C023 — Cumulative disclosure-budget / differencing guard
**Provisional privacy transfer.** Small-community research privacy must be audited across the **joint history of overlapping aggregate releases**, not one table at a time.

Individually anonymous-looking releases can jointly reveal a person's sensitive value through:
- before/after membership changes;
- total/subgroup complements;
- overlapping demographic/role cells;
- repeated monthly/quarterly aggregates;
- qualitative details combined with quantitative tables.

Operational core:
- maintain a release ledger;
- run differencing/reconstruction checks before publication;
- do not rely on minimum-cell rules alone;
- prefer pooled/federation-level public reporting for sensitive tiny-N outcomes;
- separate private local feedback, federation research access, and public release;
- consider prior/future releases and auxiliary knowledge members already possess;
- use formal differential privacy only when its noise/complexity tradeoff is justified, and manage composition over time.

Intentional-community research already recognizes small-community re-identification risk; the surviving transfer is **composition-aware cumulative disclosure control** for repeated federation reporting.

Full audit: `runs/2026-08-15-orthogonal-tail-batch-47.md`.

## Method findings

- **M001:** formalization is not novelty.
- **M002:** user familiarity veto dominates model confidence.
- **M003:** nearest-neighbor attack is mandatory before promotion.
- **M004:** nonstandard structural cross-domain transfer can count; same-domain rediscovery cannot.
- **M005:** empty batches are successful.
- **M006:** terminology changes do not rescue an existing structure.
- **M007:** mathematically surprising but materially irrelevant transfers stay demoted.
- **M008:** originality and practical usefulness require separate dispositions.
- **M009:** known source mechanisms survive only when the target transfer changes an actual design/test/measurement decision.
- **M010:** when a broad analogy collides, narrow until a distinct operational object remains or the branch dies.
- **M011:** accountability and adversarial unpredictability can coexist through commit-before-randomness + replay-after-selection.
- **M012:** intervention-triggering predictions require intervention-aware evaluation.
- **M013:** negative institutional knowledge must propagate through replication channels, not merely archives.
- **M014:** weighted federations must design power under the complete rule, not visible weights.
- **M015:** reproduction can carry an implicit federation political subsidy/penalty.
- **M016:** when governance attention changes observability, observation regime is part of evidence provenance.
- **M017:** empirical comparison needs a measurement-comparability gate before pooling.
- **M018:** small-N confidentiality is a property of the whole release history and adversary knowledge, not of each aggregate release independently.
