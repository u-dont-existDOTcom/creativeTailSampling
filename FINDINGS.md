## Current survivors

### C001 — Active normative edge-case search
**Provisional cross-domain transfer.** Use active-learning logic to select concrete governance/value cases most likely to distinguish plausible interpretations; collect independent answers before deliberation and preserve high-information resolved cases as constitutional regression tests.

### C003 — Asset-gated cultural mutation
**Narrow provisional survivor.** Collective branch inheritance can pre-filter which institutional variants receive enough land/capital/reputation to become viable competitors, making canonical-form superiority partly a pre-selection capitalization effect.

### C005 — Reproductive-variance / superstar-reproduction trap
**Empirically strengthened.** Reproducibility requires the parent-offspring distribution, not total spread or mean daughters alone. Track `P(K=0)`, median, variance/mean, top-decile share, generation interval, granddaughter reproduction, and subsidy/propagule structure.

Mature-parent Hutterite signatures:
- Manitoba Schmiedeleut: `P(K=0)=4.08%`, median `K=2`, top-10% share ~22.3%.
- Lehrerleut 1918–1953: `P(K=0)=3.45%`, median `K=1`, top-10% share 24.53%.
- Dariusleut 1918–1953: `P(K=0)=0%`, median `K=2`, top-10% share 20.0%.

Operational shorthand: **make reproduction boring**.

### C006 — Descendant inflation / lineage-size sampling bias
**Research control.** Prolific lineages mechanically contribute more extant observations. State whether the estimand is a random founding attempt/root, lineage, extant community, current resident, or future movement contribution.

### C009 — Cohort-composition admission
**Provisional; market-thickness bounded.** Applicant value can be non-separable. Use set-level admission analysis only when several plausible applicants/openings genuinely overlap—especially founding, daughter formation, new sites, and expansion waves.

### C011 — Shadow governance
**Provisional cross-domain transfer.** Before transferring authority to a substantially new governance process, run it nonbinding in parallel on the same admissible cases and analyze divergence before live adoption.

### C012 — Standing constitutional-relation testing
**Provisional, narrowed.** Maintain versioned tests for relations the constitution claims should hold—symmetry, irrelevance/invariance, monotonicity, jurisdiction invariance, etc.—not merely individual example answers.

### C013 — Federated reciprocal applicant clearing after exploration
**Narrow provisional transfer.** After visits/trials reveal reciprocal preferences, coordinate commitments movement-wide so compatible seeker–vacancy pairs are not lost to isolated timing/search decisions, preserving opt-out and local admission authority.

### C015 — Governance rights-liveness verification
**Provisional transfer from formal methods.** A right can exist in text yet be dead in a reachable state. Verify safety, liveness, and bounded liveness under realistic recusal, vacancy, quorum, jurisdiction, deadline, delegation, escalation, strategic-noncooperation, and capacity states; expose every cooperation/fairness assumption.

### C016 — Verifiably unpredictable federation audit sampling
**Provisional transfer.** When random process/financial/quality auditing is independently justified: publish audit rights/scope; freeze the eligible universe before future randomness is knowable; precommit deterministic selection; use uncontrollable future randomness; and make the realized draw replayable afterward.

### C017 — Intervention-aware rescue forecasts
**Provisional transfer.** Do not use the intervention-produced outcome as an unadjusted label for a forecast that triggered the intervention. Define the forecast's intervention regime, freeze pre-rescue evidence, separate forecast quality from rescue efficacy, and record rescue/subsidy exposure.

Full audit: `runs/2026-08-15-orthogonal-tail-batch-23.md`.

### C018 — Replicated-governance tombstones
**Provisional transfer.** When a high-consequence governance pattern is retired for a known failure, propagate a semantic deprecation record through the same daughter/template/fork channels that could otherwise resurrect it. Tombstones force explicit reconsideration; they are not eternal bans.

Full audit: `runs/2026-08-15-orthogonal-tail-batch-25.md`.

### C019 — Power-targeted federation voting design
**Provisional transfer from voting-power theory.** Design/audit the **power distribution intended**, not the visible weight vector. Define the representation objective; model local and federation tiers; compute pivotal power under weights + quota; stress correlated/bloc/delegate behavior; solve weights and quota jointly toward the target; re-audit after structural change.

Do **not** reduce this to `use square-root weights`.

Reusable tool: `analysis/weighted_voting_power.py`.

Full audit: `runs/2026-08-15-orthogonal-tail-batch-30.md`.

### C020 — Fission/merge representation-effect audit
**Provisional transfer linking voting power to communal reproduction.** Legitimate daughter formation/merger can create an accidental political subsidy or penalty. Calculate before/after power and use an aligned-preference split/merge counterfactual to isolate how much influence changes solely because the organizational boundary changed. A non-zero autonomy/community-unit premium may be legitimate, but it should be explicit.

Full audit: `runs/2026-08-15-orthogonal-tail-batch-31.md`.

### C021 — Scrutiny-conditioned evidence accounting
**Provisional transfer from selective-label/surveillance-feedback research.** When suspicion changes how intensely a person is observed, later evidence must preserve the observation regime. Raw monitoring-discovered incident counts cannot be treated as independent confirmation of the suspicion that caused the extra scrutiny.

Operational core:
- distinguish independent reports, routine observations, and suspicion-triggered discoveries;
- record when/why observation intensity changed;
- keep trigger evidence distinct from monitoring-generated evidence;
- treat low observation as missing information rather than zero risk;
- use standardized role-based controls for sensitive powers where feasible;
- preserve severe direct evidence rather than mechanically normalizing it away;
- at escalation review, ask how much evidence would have existed under ordinary observation;
- account for restrictions/monitoring that also change behavior/opportunity.

Full audit: `runs/2026-08-15-orthogonal-tail-batch-34.md`.

## Method findings

- **M001:** formalization is not novelty.
- **M002:** user familiarity veto dominates model confidence.
- **M003:** nearest-neighbor attack is mandatory before promotion.
- **M004:** nonstandard structural cross-domain transfer can count; same-domain rediscovery cannot.
- **M005:** empty batches are successful.
- **M006:** terminology changes do not rescue an existing structure.
- **M007:** mathematically surprising but materially irrelevant transfers stay demoted.
- **M008:** originality and practical usefulness require separate dispositions.
- **M009:** known source mechanisms survive only when the target transfer changes an actual design/test/measurement decision.
- **M010:** when a broad analogy collides, narrow until a distinct operational object remains or the branch dies.
- **M011:** accountability and adversarial unpredictability can coexist through commit-before-randomness + replay-after-selection.
- **M012:** intervention-triggering predictions require intervention-aware evaluation.
- **M013:** negative institutional knowledge must propagate through replication channels, not merely archives.
- **M014:** weighted federations must design power under the complete rule, not visible weights.
- **M015:** reproduction can carry an implicit federation political subsidy/penalty.
- **M016:** when governance attention changes observability, the observation regime is part of evidence provenance.
