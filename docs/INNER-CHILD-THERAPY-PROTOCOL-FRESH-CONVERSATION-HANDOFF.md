## Canonical architecture

The protocol is no longer just a direct inner-child state machine. It has:

1. a thin actor/problem-class/current-reality router;
2. condition-specific outer safety and authority maps;
3. the inner-child/reparenting subprotocol;
4. outcome, burden, adverse-effect, and longitudinal-safety loops.

The bot first asks whether the requested help is for the user, another adult, or a dependent; whether current danger/basic needs/medical instability override ordinary work; and whether inner work is primary, adjunctive, deferred, or irrelevant to the next action.

## Owner-locked rules

- One inner parent / integrated adult with three qualities: nurturing, protecting, guiding.
- Do not construct three autonomous inner parents.
- Depth and integration are independent axes.
- Internalization of the reparenting function is not human self-sufficiency.
- Felt sense **may** recover what conditioning obscured; it does not establish history by itself.
- Poor outcome enters a differential rather than an immediate broad-model conclusion.
