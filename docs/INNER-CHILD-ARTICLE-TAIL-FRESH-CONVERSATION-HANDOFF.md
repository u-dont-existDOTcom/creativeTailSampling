## Authoritative owner corrections from Batch 001

Do not repeat the following mistakes.

### A. Outcome measurement / falsifiability

Yes, outcomes should be measured and a protocol can be wrong in some respect. But do **not** jump from `no results yet` to `reparenting is wrong for this person`.

The more likely differential includes:
- misunderstanding or incorrect use;
- insufficient repetition, dose, or duration;
- wrong sequencing or missing prerequisite support;
- a delivery technique that does not fit;
- the protocol needing more nuance or updating;
- another therapy/adjunct being needed for the present obstacle.

My thesis is that if the inner-child / inner-adult relationship is dysfunctional, some form of reparenting is ultimately necessary. Other therapies can help, but they do not eliminate that target. You may research or challenge that thesis explicitly; do not silently replace it with a generic `switch methods if it hasn't worked yet` claim.

The useful tail question is therefore how to discriminate **implementation failure, sequencing failure, protocol incompleteness, adjunct need, and genuine mechanism failure** rather than treating them as one category.

### B. Depth of experience vs integration

Do **not** redefine depth as integration.

In this article, **depth of experience** is about richness / degree of contact with the material and may include access and/or intensity, usually both. **Integration** is a separate downstream dimension.

A deep experience can be poorly integrated. A less intense experience can integrate well. The nighttime/dream passage is making a claim about deeper experiential contact, not claiming that intensity itself proves better integration or clinical efficacy.

The previous analysis misplaced the categories here.

### C. Internalization vs interdependence

Do **not** imply that the sequence

`receive care → observe care → participate → initiate → internalize`

is wrong merely because adults can remain interdependent.

This is an article on **reparenting**. Internalization of the caregiving/adult function is the point. A person can internalize that function and still continue to receive therapy, friendship, community support, practical support, etc.

So keep three concepts distinct:
- internalization of the reparenting function;
- healthy ongoing interdependence;
- authority dependency.

An addition about interdependence is only useful if it does not erase the required internalization step.

### D. Felt sense

Preserve the existing sentence that the felt sense **may know what the conditioned self was trained to forget**.

The word `may` is already epistemically qualified and intentional. Do not silently weaken it into `felt sense only reports present experience`.

The article already contains strong distinctions among memory, testimony, inference, imagery, dream, altered-state material, emotional truth, and historical fact. Additional safeguards are acceptable only if genuinely additive and not used to erase the modal claim.
