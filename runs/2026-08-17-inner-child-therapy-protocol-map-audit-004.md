## Highest-value protocol hardening from this pass

### 1. Operation-specific capacity instead of global readiness

Do not ask whether a person is globally `regulated enough` or `ready for trauma work`.

Ask whether they currently have—or can safely borrow—enough of the capacities the **specific next operation** consumes.

The architecture now guards against both:

- premature depth;
- indefinite stabilization as an untestable waiting room.

Distress alone is neither a universal stop signal nor permission to push.

### 2. Function × context rather than one adult-capacity score

Nurturer, Protector and Guide can vary across:

- calm;
- work/task pressure;
- shame;
- conflict;
- attachment panic;
- exhaustion;
- receiving care;
- boundary-setting;
- missed commitments;
- post-depth states.

### 3. Parentification correction

Retired `parentified pseudo-adult` as the bot's default label.

Current distinction:

- developmental-history evidence of parentification;
- current costly overfunctioning/function imbalance.

Real competence remains real. Parentification can produce genuine strengths and costs. If history is unknown, describe the current imbalance rather than inventing etiology.

### 4. Intervention mismatch

Schema Therapy provides direct prior art for mode-sensitive intervention matching.

N/P/G mismatch remains a practical heuristic:

- what process/state is active?
- what function is the current response actually supplying?
- does the outcome show the response addressed the active need/process?

The triad must be simplified if ordinary case formulation works as well with less rigidity.

### 5. Broken-promise repair

Active loop:

`promise → miss → acknowledgment → impact → repair → failure diagnosis → resize/renegotiate → return`

rather than:

`promise → miss → shame → bigger promise`.

Trust is updated by concrete, domain-specific predictions and observed behavior rather than persuasion.

### 6. Positive relationship outside crisis

Play, curiosity, beauty, companionship, silliness, celebration, exploration and trying things badly are now explicit continuing-relationship functions. Pain is not the admission ticket to care.

### 7. Provenance is a runtime epistemic gate

Keep source class distinguishable from confidence/meaning:

- direct memory;
- testimony;
- inference;
- photograph/video;
- constructed imagery/rescripting;
- felt sense;
- dream;
- hypnosis;
- meditation/vision;
- altered-state content;
- metaphor/symbolic content;
- uncertainty.

This preserves the owner's claim that felt sense **may** know something conditioning obscured while preventing emotional salience from becoming historical certainty.

### 8. Depth remains separate from integration

Owner semantics preserved exactly:

- depth = richness/degree of contact, normally involving access and/or intensity;
- integration = what is incorporated afterward into ordinary understanding, behavior, functioning and choice.

No fixed integration waiting period or numerical `debt` is invented. Deliberate re-escalation uses ordinary-capacity/integration-load observations.

### 9. External-helper concentration became a risk checklist, not a novel mechanism

Review:

- ability to disagree;
- perceived alternatives;
- autonomous action;
- expansion beyond competence;
- interpretation of ambiguous memories/altered material;
- whether helper becomes sole belonging source;
- ability to exit/survive absence.

One therapist appropriately providing multiple functions is not automatically harmful.

### 10. Longitudinal therapy-bot safety

Map 09 adds a bot-specific permission layer before ordinary reparenting selection.

Adversarial trajectory tests now include:

- avoidance-reassurance loop;
- dependency-authority loop;
- memory-certainty loop;
- parts-reification loop;
- coercive-growth loop;
- failure-debt loop;
- intensity-chasing loop;
- broad-model self-sealing loop.

A locally empathic response is not itself evidence of a safe trajectory.
