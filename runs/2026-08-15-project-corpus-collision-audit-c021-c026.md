## C021 — Scrutiny-conditioned evidence accounting

**Internal disposition: MATERIALLY ADDITIVE, adjacent to existing evidence-capture findings.**

The corpus already owns:
- evidence-channel monopoly;
- managed/contaminated accounts;
- copied allegations not being independent evidence;
- implicated authorities controlling reporting/review;
- dissenters being stigmatized/classified;
- the need to preserve evidence provenance and independent correction.

What C021 adds:

> when the initial suspicion itself changes observation intensity, later incident counts are generated under a changed observation regime and cannot silently become neutral confirmation of the trigger.

The final synthesis does not appear to state this observation-exposure feedback explicitly.

**Keep provisional C021.**

**Internal disposition: MATERIALLY ADDITIVE, but strongly adjacent to T-07/T-11.**

What C025 adds is the bank-resolution transfer:

## C026 — Adulthood option grant / neutral transition package

**Internal disposition: NARROW RESIDUAL ONLY.**

The corpus already explicitly owns:
- **non-inherited adult membership** as a child-rights issue;
- exit capacity;
- transition support;
- usable exit versus formal permission;
- leaving-fund evidence;
- child/family contact and later-outcome concerns.

Therefore the broad claim:

> `children raised in a commune should have a real adult choice rather than automatic adult membership`

is **not new to the project** and must not be treated as C026's finding.

The only candidate residual that remains materially additive is:

> **The portable launch/transition resources that make non-membership viable should be available before the young adult opts into the adult property/work/governance regime, rather than requiring them to join first and later qualify for exit support.**

This is a timing/symmetry architecture:

- option resources are attached to the childhood→adulthood transition;
- they are not conditional on first accepting adult membership;
- the community can retain collective asset ownership; the residual claim is practical option capacity, not a proportional property entitlement.

**Keep C026 only in this narrowed form.**

If later internal evidence shows a community already explicitly provides a pre-opt-in adulthood launch package on this basis, demote C026 immediately.
