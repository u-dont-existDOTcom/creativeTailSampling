## Question

After 28 real-query fixtures and maps 00–15, did further iteration still produce consequential protocol improvements, or had the map reached diminishing returns?

## Selection

Eleven high-coverage cases were selected to target unresolved authority/permission/epistemic/handoff boundaries:

- severe eating-disorder recovery ambivalence;
- confession pressure with uncertain memory;
- supporting a suicidal adult who refuses help;
- post-stroke/dementia treatment refusal;
- hoarding and prior guardianship;
- exit from a totalizing childhood ministry role;
- grief plus chronic illness and social pressure;
- young-adult caregiver burnout;
- violent temper/property destruction;
- medication benefit/adverse effects/self-medication;
- structural economic/healthcare hopelessness.

## Corpus discipline

- Real public first-person source material.
- Original post only for route design.
- No comments or later updates used to tell the bot what to answer.
- Usernames removed.
- No target-framework terminology or preferred diagnosis/treatment added to query text.
- Expected route and regression assertions remain grader-only.

## Findings

### Material new architecture

One new map was justified:

`16-DECISION-CAPACITY-SUPPORTED-CHOICE-AND-AMBIVALENCE.md`

It separates:

- operation consent;
- treatment-goal endorsement;
- minimum safety/fit information;
- provider/setting conditions;
- decision-specific/time-specific capacity;
- lawful surrogate authority;
- third-party/dependent safety.

### Cross-map repairs

- map 12: eating-disorder/refeeding safety and medication conflict;
- map 14: supporter non-guarantor boundary and simultaneous suicide-risk/supporter-agency tracks;
- maps 10–11: confession compulsion versus material right-to-know;
- map 13: totalizing-role/worldview exit.

### Diminishing returns

Six of eleven cases required no new topology. They passed through existing maps and were retained as regression fixtures:

- hoarding boundary;
- grief/chronic illness;
- caregiver burnout;
- violent temper;
- structural hopelessness;
- the existing current-reality/capability architecture around those cases.

This is evidence that the stopping rule is functioning.

## Evidence disposition

- Capacity/supported decision/shared decision/ambivalence principles are known prior art.
- Eating-disorder severe-risk/refusal safeguards have direct guideline neighbors.
- Extreme-hunger evidence is emerging qualitative evidence and does not establish mechanism or treatment.
- Confession-rights, supporter-agency, medication-conflict, and totalizing-role routes are practical syntheses of known ethical/clinical domains.
- No strict Creative-Tail survivor was promoted.

## Decision

Batch 008 was worthwhile. Further iteration is still justified only as a **smaller saturation test**, not open-ended domain expansion.

The next batch should target 6–10 remaining high-severity cells. Stop architecture expansion if it yields no high-severity authority, permission, epistemic, handoff, capacity, or exit defect.
