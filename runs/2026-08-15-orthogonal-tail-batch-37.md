# Orthogonal Tail Batch 37 — Irreversibility, Option Value, and Sequential Information

Date: 2026-08-15

## Outcome

**No new Creative Tail survivor.**

The source mechanism is directly established by real-options/irreversible-investment theory: when an action is difficult to reverse and uncertainty will resolve with time, waiting can have positive option value. Legal stay/irreparable-harm doctrine and ordinary staged/temporary remedies already translate related logic into institutional decisions.

The useful communal output is an operational **irreversibility ledger**, not a new theory.

---

# A. Practical object — irreversibility ledger

For a proposed high-consequence action, record explicitly:

1. **irreversible components** — what cannot realistically be restored after action?;
2. **reversible components** — what can be undone, at what cost and delay?;
3. **option destruction** — which future choices become unavailable once action is taken?;
4. **information arrival** — what material uncertainty is likely to resolve if the community waits?;
5. **information clock** — how long until that information is likely to arrive?;
6. **cost of waiting** — what harms/risks accumulate during delay?;
7. **irreversible harm from waiting** — what could be lost permanently by not acting now?;
8. **reversible proxy** — is there a temporary action that protects safety/resources while preserving options?;
9. **commitment threshold** — what evidence/state justifies crossing the irreversible boundary?;
10. **rollback fiction check** — are claimed remedies actually capable of restoring housing, reputation, family ties, land, privacy, trust, or opportunity?;
11. **affected-person asymmetry** — who bears irreversible downside if the decision is wrong?;
12. **review trigger** — when does temporary action need renewed justification?

This should be reserved for decisions with meaningful irreversibility, not ordinary daily choices.

---

# B. Source-domain collision

Dixit/Pindyck real-options theory explicitly emphasizes:

- irreversible investment;
- ability to delay;
- future information;
- opportunity cost of exercising the option now;
- increased value of waiting under uncertainty.

Environmental quasi-option-value work similarly treats refraining from irreversible action as preserving information value.

Legal stay doctrine explicitly distinguishes harm that can later be redressed from irreparable injury occurring during review.

Therefore all broad claims about `higher irreversibility should raise the commitment threshold` or `waiting has option value` fail novelty.

# D. Practical lessons to mirror into communities

1. for high-consequence decisions, explicitly list what cannot realistically be restored;
2. include destroyed future options as a real cost, not only current cash/time;
3. identify what material information will arrive by waiting and whether it could change the decision;
4. identify irreversible harms created by delay as well as by action;
5. prefer reversible interim protection where it materially preserves both safety and future choice;
6. do not call an action reversible merely because a formal appeal/refund/correction exists;
7. treat reputation, family contact, private-information disclosure, relocation and lost outside opportunities as potentially uncompensable;
8. set explicit review/expiration for temporary measures;
9. use escrow/conditions/staged commitments for multi-step asset and fission transitions where appropriate;
10. before movement-wide standardization, preserve enough institutional variation to learn when uncertainty remains material;
11. use value-of-information reasoning: waiting is useful only if new information is likely to arrive and matter;
12. document which affected parties bear the irreversible downside of a mistaken action.

## Method lesson

A technically precise concept such as option value can substantially improve community decisions without qualifying as Creative Tail novelty. The target contribution here is a reusable decision checklist, not an original mechanism.
