# Orthogonal Tail Batch 42 — Constituency-Mutating Decisions / Endogenous Electorates

Date: 2026-08-15

## Outcome

**No Creative Tail survivor.**

Dynamic voting in clubs already studies organizations where current members vote on decisions that change the future membership/electorate. Electoral-integrity and constitutional-entrenchment literatures also directly address manipulation of membership/electorate composition to entrench current power.

The target application remains highly practical for intentional communities.

---

# Core problem

Some communal decisions are not ordinary policy decisions because they alter **who will have authority to review or reverse them later**.

Examples:
- admitting a bloc of new members;
- expelling/suspending members;
- changing voting eligibility;
- changing probation-to-membership rules;
- changing household/member vote definitions;
- fission/merger;
- changing delegate selection;
- changing the body that appoints reviewers/appeal officers.

These are **constituency-mutating decisions**.

A temporary majority can use them to make itself a future majority, or can unintentionally lock in a governance direction because the post-change constituency has different preferences from the pre-change constituency.

## Literature collision

- Dynamic-voting/club theory explicitly models endogenous electorates where members decide membership.
- Electoral-integrity research on political organizations identifies membership-roll manipulation, selective eligibility and incumbency control as internal-election risks.
- Gerrymandering/court-packing/constitutional-entrenchment literatures address related self-entrenchment mechanisms.

**Disposition: no novelty claim.**

---

# Practical controls

## B42-L01 — Flag constituency-mutating decisions explicitly

Do not process them as routine policy motions.

The agenda should state:
- how the decision changes future decision-maker composition;
- which later decisions become easier/harder;
- whether pending votes/disputes are affected.

## B42-L02 — Freeze the electorate for pending contested decisions

Where appropriate, define eligibility/cutoff before the substantive contest begins so a faction cannot add/remove decisive voters mid-process.

This needs exceptions for ordinary rights and cannot become a device for permanently excluding legitimate members.

## B42-L03 — Separate member-safety decisions from factional vote arithmetic

A serious conduct-based restriction/expulsion cannot be blocked merely because it changes a coalition balance.

Conversely, voting consequences should not become the hidden reason for a purported safety decision.

Require conduct-specific evidence, recusal and review.

## B42-L04 — Record pre/post constituency counterfactual

For major structural changes ask:
- who can vote before?;
- who can vote after?;
- how would the pending/likely key decisions differ under each electorate?;
- does the structural decision create a predictable political beneficiary?

A beneficiary is not proof of illegitimacy; it is a conflict/entrenchment diagnostic.

## B42-L05 — Use heightened process for rules governing membership itself

Possible safeguards depending on context:
- supermajority;
- advance notice;
- independent facilitation/review;
- fixed transition date;
- post-change confirmation;
- affected-member reply;
- federation appeal;
- sunset/review.

No one safeguard is universally correct.

## B42-L06 — Do not let newly created authority validate its own creation retroactively

Where practicable, the procedure that creates a new governing/review body should be validated under the pre-existing legitimate rules, not only by the body after it exists.

## B42-L07 — Distinguish genuine growth from membership packing

Fast admissions can be legitimate growth.

Diagnostic questions:
- was the admission pipeline ordinary/preannounced?;
- were standards changed selectively?;
- were applicants recruited around a pending vote?;
- were existing dissenters simultaneously suspended/expelled?;
- did admission timing determine the outcome?;
- would the same process be acceptable if the factional advantage reversed?

## B42-L08 — Treat fission/merger as constituency change as well as asset/reproduction change

Connect to C020:
- federation voting power changes;
- local electorates change;
- minority/majority status can reverse;
- review/appeal jurisdiction may change.

Fission planning should model these political transitions alongside assets and households.

## B42-L09 — Membership-roll integrity belongs in governance audit

For formal votes preserve:
- eligible-member list and cutoff;
- additions/removals;
- reason/legal basis;
- conflicts/appeals;
- proxy/delegate status.

Do not create unnecessary surveillance for ordinary consensus processes; focus on decisions where formal eligibility matters.

## B42-L10 — Post-change legitimacy matters too

A rule validly adopted by the old constituency can become unacceptable to the new constituency.

Where a major structural decision deliberately creates a materially new political body, a later confirmation/review can distinguish legitimate succession from permanent dead-hand control by predecessors.

## B42-L11 — Entrenchment can occur through care/housing dependency, not only formal votes

A coalition may not need to change official franchise if it controls who can realistically remain housed, employed, partnered, or socially viable in the community.

Connect constituency analysis to practical-power mapping (community lessons L001/L064), not only member rolls.

## B42-L12 — Research center should mark membership composition as endogenous

When evaluating a governance system, membership composition is partly an outcome of the system itself.

Do not control it away casually as if it were an exogenous background variable.

---

## Disposition

No Creative Tail survivor.

Practical rule:

> **Treat any decision that changes the future decision-maker population as a reflexive constitutional decision, not ordinary policy. Audit who gains future review/reversal power and protect against selective constituency manipulation.**
