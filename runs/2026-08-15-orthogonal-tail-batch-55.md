# Orthogonal Tail Batch 55 — Portable Safety Information Without Federation Blacklisting

Date: 2026-08-15

## Outcome

**No Creative Tail survivor.**

Safeguarding, employment-reference, data-rights and professional-regulation systems already confront the central problem: organizations sometimes need to share substantiated risk information, while indiscriminate transmission of allegations can create severe reputational harm and informal blacklisting.

The communal federation implementation is still high-value.

---

# Core problem

A federation creates two opposite risks:

1. **information silo:** a genuinely dangerous pattern is known in Community A but Community B receives no warning when the person moves;
2. **reputation cascade:** Community A's accusation, interpersonal conflict, or low-quality finding becomes a movement-wide exclusion label copied by every other community.

The solution should not be `share nothing` or `share everything`.

## Literature / policy collision

Safeguarding guidance explicitly distinguishes allegation outcomes such as:
- substantiated;
- unsubstantiated;
- unfounded;
- malicious;
- false.

Some official reference guidance states that substantiated allegations can be included in future references while unsubstantiated/unfounded/malicious/false allegations should not be carried into future references.

Safeguarding record guidance also emphasizes separating facts, opinions and judgments, recording rationale, restricting access, and sharing information where necessary for safety.

Data-protection guidance provides correction/access rights subject to safeguarding exceptions.

**Disposition: no novelty claim.**

---

# Practical architecture

## B55-L01 — Never transmit a one-word person label

Avoid portable labels such as:
- `unsafe`;
- `psychopath`;
- `bad fit`;
- `toxic`;
- `do not admit`.

Transmit bounded information about:
- observed conduct;
- evidence state;
- role/context;
- current risk control;
- procedural outcome.

## B55-L02 — Use explicit epistemic states

Every portable concern should state one of a controlled set such as:
- allegation pending;
- substantiated risk/finding;
- unsubstantiated / insufficient evidence;
- unfounded;
- false;
- malicious;
- resolved/expired restriction;
- finding overturned/corrected.

Definitions must be federation-governed rather than improvised locally.

## B55-L03 — Facts, reports, interpretation and decision are separate fields

Example packet:
- original report/source;
- directly observed facts;
- corroboration/dependencies;
- alternative account;
- finding/risk assessment;
- restriction/decision;
- appeal/correction state.

Do not flatten the packet into the conclusion.

## B55-L04 — Observation regime travels with the evidence (C021)

If an incident count was generated under intensified monitoring, the receiving community needs to know:
- when monitoring increased;
- why;
- what ordinary observation baseline was;
- which evidence predated the scrutiny.

Otherwise surveillance-generated evidence can become laundered as neutral portable reputation.

## B55-L05 — Scope warnings to the relevant role/risk

A finding can justify restriction from:
- childcare;
- finances;
- private records;
- driving;
- medical/care authority

without establishing that the person is globally unsafe for residence, friendship, work, or governance.

Use role-specific controls where possible.

## B55-L06 — The receiving community makes its own decision

Portable evidence should inform rather than automatically dictate local admission except where the federation constitution explicitly establishes a binding safeguarding bar for a defined role/risk.

Local independent review reduces cascade error.

## B55-L07 — Correction/reversal must propagate downstream

Connect to Batch 26.

If the source finding is:
- corrected;
- overturned;
- reclassified;
- expired,

the federation system should identify every downstream recipient that relied on the old version and push the update.

## B55-L08 — Version portable records

Every shared packet needs:
- source community;
- date/version;
- current status;
- review/expiry date;
- correction history;
- recipient/share log.

Stale screenshots and copied notes should not become permanent unofficial blacklists.

## B55-L09 — Give the subject appropriate access/reply/correction rights

Subject to legitimate victim/safeguarding confidentiality, the person should generally know:
- what portable finding exists;
- how to challenge factual errors;
- current restriction/status;
- review route.

Do not disclose information where doing so would foreseeably endanger a protected person or violate law.

## B55-L10 — Do not transport irrelevant interpersonal dislike as safety information

Separate:
- incompatibility;
- political disagreement;
- relationship conflict;
- ordinary poor fit

from evidence of a portable safety/rights risk.

## B55-L11 — Repeated low-quality allegations require provenance analysis, not simple counting

Ten copied allegations from one originating conflict are not ten independent concerns.

Connect to evidence-lineage counting and C021 observation effects.

## B55-L12 — Severe direct evidence should not be diluted by procedural formalism

A portable system exists partly because genuine predatory behavior can exploit organizational boundaries.

Strong direct evidence of serious risk should trigger proportionate interim protection while review proceeds.

## B55-L13 — Limit destination access

Portable risk packets should be available only to people who need them for relevant decisions, not searchable as movement gossip.

## B55-L14 — Audit disparate use

Track whether portable concern systems disproportionately:
- follow unpopular/dissenting members;
- encode local factional conflicts;
- affect certain demographic groups;
- become a de facto exclusion mechanism without formal adjudication.

## B55-L15 — Transfers should not erase legitimate restrictions

Batch 46 private transfer infrastructure must not allow someone to evade a substantiated safeguarding restriction by changing communities.

The private search stage can remain confidential, while a formal placement stage can apply relevant verified role restrictions.

## B55-L16 — Unsubstantiated does not always mean `nothing happened`

Insufficient evidence is not proof of innocence or guilt.

The federation must distinguish:
- what can lawfully/ethically be carried forward;
- whether a temporary risk control remains justified;
- what cannot be represented as a proven fact.

---

## Disposition

No Creative Tail survivor.

Strong practical rule:

> **A federation should transport structured, scoped, versioned evidence and risk decisions—not portable moral identities. The observation regime, evidentiary status, appeal/correction history and later reversals must travel with the record, and receiving communities should ordinarily make their own bounded decision.**
