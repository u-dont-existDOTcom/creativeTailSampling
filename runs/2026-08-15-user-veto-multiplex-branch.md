## Why the branch failed

The root proposition and its descendants occupy a heavily developed intellectual neighborhood:

- Marx: commodity relations, alienation, division of labor and indirect social relations;
- Durkheim: mechanical versus organic solidarity / division of labor;
- Tönnies: Gemeinschaft versus Gesellschaft;
- Polanyi: embedded versus disembedded economic relations;
- social-capital and embeddedness traditions;
- modern multiplex-network research explicitly studying overlap of social domains.

A targeted literature screen additionally found modern research explicitly connecting multiplex network overlap to Durkheim's mechanical/organic solidarity distinction.

Therefore increasingly technical consequences such as capability atrophy, common-access coupling, combinatorial replaceability, hysteresis, or relational option value do not qualify as Creative Tail Sampling discoveries merely because those exact labels are unusual.

## Protocol lesson

The earlier protocol allowed a false-positive path:

1. start with a familiar root idea;
2. derive several steps;
3. give the downstream mechanisms technical names;
4. mistake distance in wording for distance in concept-space.

New rule: once the root branch compresses into an existing theory/tradition, stop elaborating it for novelty. A candidate must survive an explicit historical/literature nearest-neighbor search before promotion.

## New rejection frontier

For community/social-structure work, do not promote claims that substantially reduce to:

- specialization commodifies or alienates people;
- modern society replaces thick/direct ties with thin/contractual ones;
- social embeddedness creates trust/support;
- multiplex ties carry resources across domains;
- specialization makes actors more interchangeable;
- markets/bureaucracies substitute for informal relations;
- community requires more role overlap;
- strong/tight relations can create both support and entrapment.

These may be premises for an orthogonal inference, but are not findings.
