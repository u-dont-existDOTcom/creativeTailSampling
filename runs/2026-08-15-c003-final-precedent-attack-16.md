# Batch 16A — Final Precedent Attack on C003 Asset-Gated Cultural Mutation

Date: 2026-08-15

## Candidate under attack

C003 is deliberately narrow:

> **Collective branch inheritance can act as a pre-selection filter on which institutional variants receive enough inherited capital to become viable competitors.**

The proposed consequence is selection blindness: observed institutional evolution compares only variants that the incumbent reproduction/property architecture allowed to become adequately capitalized organizations.

## Nearest neighbors found

The final targeted search found several close literatures:

1. **Organizational spin-offs** — new organizations inherit routines, capabilities, resources, ownership structures, and other parent characteristics; those inherited endowments affect performance and survival.
2. **Religious/organizational schism** — schism is a major mechanism generating organizational and doctrinal diversity.
3. **Church-property disputes after schism** — legal/denominational structures determine whether breakaway congregations retain buildings and other property or leave without them.
4. **Property rights and innovation** — ownership/control of strategic assets can affect innovation incentives and outcomes.

These neighbors eliminate any broader claim that resources, property, inheritance, or schism affect organizational evolution.

## What was not located

The targeted searches did not locate a close precedent explicitly combining the pieces as:

> the **capital-inheritance rule for organizational branches is part of the variation-generation operator itself**, because it determines which cultural/institutional variants become sufficiently resourced to enter subsequent selection.

Absence from this search is not evidence of academic originality.

## Verdict

**FREEZE C003 AS A NARROW PROVISIONAL CROSS-DOMAIN CONNECTION.**

Do not search or elaborate it further unless new evidence arises naturally.

Future use must preserve the narrow claim and must not expand it into familiar propositions such as:

- property affects exit;
- property affects innovation;
- breakaway groups fight over assets;
- well-funded organizations survive more easily;
- schism generates diversity.

## Demotion condition

If later research naturally surfaces work explicitly treating collective branch asset inheritance/capital portability as a pre-selection filter on the institutional variants exposed to evolutionary competition, demote C003 immediately.

## Method lesson

A candidate can remain provisionally interesting after a bounded nearest-neighbor search without being declared unprecedented. The correct stopping rule is **freeze**, not indefinite novelty hunting.
