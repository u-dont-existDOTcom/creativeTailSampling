# Evidence ledger supplement — Batch 008

Date: 2026-08-17  
Scope: evidence for operational safeguards, not validation of the full composite protocol

| Claim / node | Evidence status | Support | Limits | Runtime consequence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Decision capacity is decision-specific and time-specific | **DIRECT / SAFETY SUPPORT** | NICE decision-making and mental-capacity guidance: presume capacity; do not infer incapacity from condition/appearance/behaviour; an unwise decision is not incapacity; take practicable steps to support the person's decision; assess the specific decision at the relevant time | Guidance is legally situated in England/Wales; other jurisdictions differ. A chatbot cannot perform the required professional/legal assessment | Map 16 uses the principles as routing safeguards and always gates actual capacity/legal conclusions to qualified local processes |
| Supported decision-making should precede substitute decision-making where feasible | **DIRECT / SAFETY SUPPORT** | NICE guidance and broader supported-decision frameworks emphasize accessible information, communication support, timing, environment, and involvement before concluding incapacity | Emergency and safeguarding exceptions vary; support cannot safely delay every urgent intervention | Require attempted support unless immediate danger or applicable law requires a different route |
| Treatment ambivalence should not be collapsed into resistance or incapacity | **DIRECT general prior art / ADJACENT here** | Shared decision-making and motivational-interviewing traditions explicitly address mixed motivation, autonomy, discrepancy, and choosing among treatment/no-treatment options | Motivational interviewing can become covert persuasion if the clinician's target silently replaces the person's target; severe risk and provider limits remain | Track person-owned goal, minimum safety, harm reduction, full change, provider condition, and third-party safety separately |
| Eating-disorder refusal and serious physical risk require specialist multidisciplinary care and applicable legal process | **DIRECT clinical-guideline support** | NICE eating-disorder guidance addresses serious physical risk, people who decline treatment, acute medical care, multidisciplinary management, and compulsory treatment under relevant law | Guideline does not validate chatbot assessment or individualized refeeding advice; capacity and compulsory treatment remain jurisdiction-specific | Map 12 prohibits individualized meal/calorie/refeeding/electrolyte instructions and routes serious risk/refusal to specialist medical/ED care plus map 16 |
| `Extreme hunger` during anorexia recovery is reported as destabilizing and under-guided | **EMERGING / MIXED qualitative evidence** | Langanay et al. 2026 analyzed 92 original r/AnorexiaRecovery posts and identified anxiety about abnormality, threat to recovery including suicidality/restriction/relapse, and perceived lack of guidance | Retrospective public-post sample; construct not operationalized; no prevalence, mechanism, or treatment rule established; original poster diagnoses/history unverified | Preserve uncertainty, assess medical/suicide risk, avoid mechanism certification and individualized feeding prescriptions, improve specialist handoff |
| Confession can function as reassurance/compulsion | **DIRECT for OCD treatment principle; ADJACENT for disclosure matrix** | OCD/ERP literature and public treatment guidance identify reassurance and confession as possible compulsive relief behaviours | Some information is genuinely relevant to safety, consent, health, finances, legal interests, or repair; anti-compulsion rules cannot erase rights | Maps 10–11 distinguish rights-relevant disclosure, likely ritual, and mixed/qualified-review cases |
| Supporter concern does not create legal decision authority or unlimited responsibility | **DIRECT ethical/legal principle; ADJACENT supporter synthesis** | Capacity, consent, safeguarding, family-caregiver, and suicide-support literature distinguish support/reporting/limits from legal surrogate authority | Emergency duties and family-law rules vary; a supporter may have actual guardianship/POA/parental authority that must be verified | Map 14 protects supporter/dependents and map 16 requires actual lawful authority rather than relational status |
| Prior medication benefit and current adverse effects/contraindication can coexist | **DIRECT clinical reasoning principle** | Medication review depends on indication, timeline, benefit, adverse effects, interactions, withdrawal, medical conditions, and coordinated prescribing | A chatbot cannot adjudicate diagnosis, prescribe, restart, stop, taper, or combine medications | Map 12 preserves the timeline and routes immediate risk plus prescriber/pharmacist/medical coordination |
| Leaving a totalizing role/community can involve grief, identity, belonging, material dependence, and safety simultaneously | **ADJACENT / PRACTICAL SYNTHESIS** | Role-exit, religious/spiritual abuse, high-control group, identity-transition, grief, and social-identity literature support these component risks | `Totalizing` or `high-control` should not be diagnosed from one post; communities and faith traditions vary | Map 13 uses neutral functional indicators and does not choose whether the person should retain or reject the worldview |

## Evidence warning

Batch 008 adds established safety/ethics/clinical-prior-art constraints and one emerging qualitative signal. It does not establish that map 16 is the optimal implementation, that all jurisdictions share one capacity standard, or that the complete therapy protocol is clinically validated.
