## Evidence boundary

The new map 15 and the Batch 007 amendments are operational hypotheses and conservative autonomy/safety constraints. They have not been validated as a unified clinical routing system. Future testing should compare them with simpler functional analysis and measure burden, false-positive routing, missed skill/medical needs, and real multi-turn outcomes.
