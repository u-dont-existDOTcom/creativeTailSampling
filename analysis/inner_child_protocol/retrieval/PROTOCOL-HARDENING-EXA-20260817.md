# 1. Internal jurisdiction / internal constitution

## Live protocol conflict

The article/protocol simultaneously says, in effect:

- listen when a child/protector/guard says no;
- do not bulldoze protection;
- the child should not run the entire external life;
- the present adult remains responsible for consequences;
- Protector has a safety function;
- Guide can require difficult but non-dangerous action;
- Nurturer care should not become payment for obedience.

The question is not whether these ideas are good separately. It is whether the bot needs an explicit constitutional division of authority to prevent contradictory routing.

## Exa query families

### IFS closest neighbor

Query:

`Internal Family Systems who makes decisions Self leadership parts advise authority consent protector veto child part decision making internal family systems therapy`

Important collision:

- IFS Institute, `The Internal Family Systems Model Outline`: Self is meant to lead; parts contribute information and ultimately respect Self leadership/decision making; the individual remains responsible for behavior. IFS process also works with protectors and pacing rather than overpowering them.

Source:

- https://ifs-institute.com/resources/articles/internal-family-systems-model-outline

This substantially collides with `parts have input / central adult integrates / adult remains responsible`.

### Schema Therapy / Healthy Adult

Query:

`schema therapy Healthy Adult Vulnerable Child Punitive Parent mode decision making limits nurture protection guidance internal child autonomy`

Important collisions:

- International Society of Schema Therapy, `Limited Reparenting`: therapist provides warmth/nurturance together with firmness, empathic confrontation and limit setting; the relationship is intended to help internalize a Healthy Adult mode.
- International Society of Schema Therapy materials on strengthening the Healthy Adult: acknowledge the child's pain, add adult hope/perspective, then deal with reality/action.
- Contemporary Healthy Adult descriptions explicitly combine nurturing/protecting vulnerable child modes, setting caring limits on impulsive/angry modes, countering punitive/demanding parent modes, and handling adult reality.

Sources:

- https://schematherapysociety.org/Limited-Reparenting/
- https://schematherapysociety.org/page-18465
- https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychiatry/articles/10.3389/fpsyt.2023.1204177/full
- https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5177643/

This is very close to the proposed Nurturer/Protector/Guide plus adult-reality allocation.

### Older vocabulary: Transactional Analysis / ego-state work

Query:

`"ego state therapy" Adult Child Parent internal decision authority nurturing parent critical parent child autonomy reparenting Transactional Analysis`

The Parent/Adult/Child architecture, Nurturing versus Critical Parent distinctions, and Adult reality-testing/mediation provide much older structural neighbors. This defeats any claim that role-differentiated internal governance is a rare transfer.

### Developmental transfer: autonomy support plus structure

Query:

`child autonomy support parental limit setting warmth authority developmental shared decision responsibility difficult tasks safety override parenting autonomy supportive limit setting`

The autonomy-supportive parenting literature repeatedly distinguishes:

- acknowledging the child's perspective/choice;
- warmth and responsiveness;
- structure/limits;
- coercive or psychologically controlling parenting.

Thus `care + voice + limits without coercion` is established developmental territory.

### Strongest falsification / reification attack

Query:

`parts therapy reification risks coercion suggestibility internal family systems protector consent criticism adult decision making dissociation fragmentation parts language`

The practical threat is that a constitution can reify metaphors into quasi-legal persons and create self-sealing interpretations (`a part disagrees, therefore the part is resisting`). Parts language must remain contestable and functional rather than ontological.

## Interim adjudication

The strong version—formal jurisdiction assigned to inner entities—is not currently justified as a new therapeutic mechanism and could add unnecessary complexity/reification.

The **minimal conflict rule** remains useful:

1. Present feelings, preferences, protest and predictions are heard as data rather than overridden or treated as pathology.
2. Optional introspective elicitation remains voluntary; clear refusal ends that elicitation for now.
3. Loss of orientation, meaningful choice or ordinary function triggers de-escalation/safety review.
4. The present adult remains responsible for external behavior and consequences.
5. Protector/Guide/Nurturer are functional lenses, not independent legal agents.
6. Nurturer non-cruelty/care remains available during disagreement or limit setting.
7. Difficult but non-dangerous action can be chosen by the present adult without using `Guide` as a license for coercive inner treatment.

# 2. Depth / altered states: cumulative integration load

## Candidate

Before deliberately escalating depth again, check whether the prior experience has been metabolized enough that ordinary orientation, choice, sleep/function, and adult capacity have substantially returned. Do not equate a brief drop in distress with completed integration.

## Exa query

`psychedelic therapy integration repeated sessions too soon integration overload destabilization spacing sessions integration period adverse effects intensity mystical experience integration outcomes`

## Evidence and collision

### Integration can be extended and destabilization can persist

`Back from the rabbit hole` (Frontiers in Psychology, 2023) describes psychedelic experiences as sometimes producing persistent distress, mood fluctuation, derealization/depersonalization, intrusive reliving and other difficult sequelae. Its two-stage integration model prioritizes support/stabilization when adverse consequences are unresolved and treats later integration as incorporation into everyday life over an open-ended period.

Source:

- https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2023.1054692/full

A qualitative practitioner study of negative effects in psilocybin-assisted interventions describes longer-term destabilization, disorientation, difficulty adapting to insights, strained ordinary functioning and uncertainty around apparent memories. It also highlights therapeutic-relationship/power complications.

Source:

- https://akjournals.com/view/journals/2054/aop/article-10.1556-2054.2024.00337/article-10.1556-2054.2024.00337.xml

A 2024 psychedelic-assisted-therapy adverse-event framework notes that actual integration duration is not established, may continue for weeks, and that adverse effects should be assessed across preparation, dosing, integration and follow-up rather than only during the intense session.

Source:

- https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/02698811241265756

### Integration frameworks are not yet empirically settled

A systematised review of 75 sources found that integration is broadly treated as important, but rigorous evidence comparing specific integration frameworks is insufficient.

Source:

- https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38148518/

### Counterweight: intensity can correlate with benefit

A 2025 systematic review/meta-analysis reports a positive association between mystical-experience intensity and clinical improvement in psychedelic-assisted treatment. This is a useful falsifier against any simplistic `more intense = worse` rule.

Source:

- https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40031999/

## Adjudication

Keep the owner distinction intact:

- intensity can sometimes correlate with benefit;
- intensity/depth is still not integration;
- unresolved post-session impairment or destabilization is a reason not to chase further access immediately;
- no evidence found justifies a universal `wait X days` rule.

### Current safe runtime version

> Before deliberately increasing experiential depth again, check whether ordinary orientation, choice, sleep/function, and access to adult capacities have substantially returned and whether unresolved high-salience material can be held without compulsive interpretation. If not, prioritize integration/recovery/support. Do not invent a universal elapsed-time threshold.

Status remains `PROVISIONAL / RESEARCH NEEDED` because the exact gate is an architecture synthesis rather than a validated treatment algorithm.

# 3. External-helper concentration

## Candidate

A single helper may become simultaneously:

- Nurturer;
- Protector;
- Guide;
- interpreter of memories/altered-state material;
- main source of belonging.

The original candidate wondered whether this bundling creates a distinctive nonlinear dependency risk.

## Exa query families

### Therapy dependency / limited reparenting / boundaries

Query:

`psychotherapy therapist dependency multiple roles authority concentration transference limited reparenting autonomy dependency one therapist source of belonging interpretation boundaries ethics`

Important collisions:

- Schema Therapy intentionally uses the therapist as a healthy model/template of care, self-control and guidance that the client eventually internalizes. Thus some concentration is not automatically pathology.
- Attachment-oriented psychotherapy conceptualizes the therapist as secure base/safe haven.
- Research on care dependency in psychotherapy treats dependency as at least partly contextual/iatrogenic and distinguishes need for therapist contact, lack of perceived alternatives and autonomous functioning.
- Psychotherapy boundary guidance explicitly asks whether an action promotes autonomous functioning or creates greater dependency, and treats multiple roles as a contextual risk rather than an automatic violation.

Sources:

- https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/cambridge-guide-to-schema-therapy/intervention-strategies-for-schema-healing-1/5DE5F34AE83B28A4881B65E8DD64370D
- https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/jclp.22043
- https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10879-022-09578-z
- https://societyforpsychotherapy.org/boundaries-and-multiple-relationships-in-psychotherapy-recommendations-for-ethical-practice/

### Undue influence / guruism / suggestibility

Query:

`psychotherapy undue influence therapist guru authority multiple roles memory interpretation belonging dependency coercive control therapist power transference spiritual authority ethics psychotherapy`

Important evidence:

- psychotherapy ethics/boundary literature treats authority, dependency, role confusion and false-memory suggestion as real safety concerns;
- psychedelic-assisted-therapy ethics literature highlights increased suggestibility/vulnerability, strong transference or `guru projections`, and risk when practitioners impose personal philosophies or become over-authoritative;
- dual-role standards warn that an added role carrying authority can increase acquiescence even when the client nominally agrees;
- spiritual-abuse literature provides a useful adverse pattern: coercive accountability, conformity pressure, unchallengeable authority and isolation.

Sources:

- https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9122134/
- https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/cambridge-quarterly-of-healthcare-ethics/article/how-to-make-psychedelicassisted-therapy-safer/48FBF66C57C389EC84D1C396E4DD55BF
- https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s12152-025-09596-3
- https://crpo.ca/practice-standards/professional-conduct/dual-relationships/
- https://researchonline.ljmu.ac.uk/id/eprint/21855/1/Responding%20well%20to%20Spiritual%20Abuse%20%20practice%20implications%20for%20counselling%20and%20psychotherapy.pdf

## Adjudication

Do **not** claim a demonstrated nonlinear bundle-specific mechanism. That wording outruns the evidence.

Retain the bundle as a **practical concentration-risk checklist** because it makes several known risk dimensions visible at once. The relevant question is not `does this helper do several things?`; it is whether concentration is accompanied by:

- reduced ability to disagree;
- reduced perceived alternatives;
- reduced autonomous action outside the relationship;
- expansion of helper authority into domains outside competence;
- helper-directed interpretation of ambiguous memories/altered material;
- social isolation or the helper becoming the only meaningful belonging source;
- difficulty exiting without threats, pathologization or loss of all support.

A single stable therapist can appropriately provide multiple relational functions. Concentration alone is neither proof of dependency nor a reason to manufacture redundant helpers.

Strict originality: **reject** the broad mechanism. Practical runtime value: **retain the checklist**.

# 4. Historical provenance / source monitoring

## Candidate

Attach a source/provenance class when experiential material first enters the protocol so that later retelling does not silently convert imagery, inference, dream, hypnosis, altered-state content or testimony into direct autobiographical memory.

## Exa query

`source monitoring false memories imagery imagination inflation psychotherapy suggestive questioning autobiographical memory provenance source monitoring framework research`

## Strong support

### Source Monitoring Framework

Johnson and colleagues' Source Monitoring Framework emphasizes that mental experiences do not carry infallible intrinsic source labels. People infer source from perceptual/contextual qualities and remembered cognitive operations. Similarity between imagined and perceived experiences, vividness, lax decision criteria, bias and missing disconfirming information can increase source errors.

Sources:

- https://memlab.yale.edu/sites/default/files/files/2000_Mitchell_Johnson_SourceMonitoring.pdf
- https://memlab.yale.edu/sites/default/files/files/2006_Johnson_AmerPsych.pdf

### Imagination inflation

Imagining childhood events can increase confidence that they happened. Imagery perspective and source-monitoring criteria influence whether imagined material is later judged as real experience.

Sources:

- https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/14704022/
- https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24213869/

### Rich autobiographical false-memory research

A meta-analysis/review of implantation paradigms discusses how guided imagery, prompts/cues, pressure and suggestion can produce rich autobiographical false memories under experimental conditions.

Source:

- https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10126919/

## Essential nuance: imagery therapy is not automatically memory implantation

A study of imagery rescripting explicitly examined the concern that imagery can alter later memory judgments. It notes laboratory imagination-inflation risks but also reports no evidence in that study that the rescripting procedure distorted voluntary memory. Important differences include making the therapeutic rescripting phase transparent, distinguishing original memory from newly imagined material, and allowing participants to generate the new imagery themselves.

Source:

- https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00426-022-01746-z

## Runtime implication

Provenance-at-ingestion is justified as a **safety/epistemic gate**, not because every internally generated image is suspect, but because source drift is a known cognitive failure mode.

Recommended conceptual provenance classes:

- direct autobiographical memory;
- testimony / something another person said;
- inference;
- photograph/video observation;
- intentionally constructed imagery/rescripting;
- bodily felt sense;
- dream;
- hypnosis/self-hypnosis;
- meditation/vision;
- entheogenic/other altered-state material;
- metaphor/symbolic content;
- uncertain/unknown.

The original class should remain inspectable after later reinterpretation. Confidence and emotional salience can be tracked separately; they do not rewrite source class.

This safeguard coexists with the owner claim that felt sense **may know what the conditioned self was trained to forget**. It regulates historical certainty; it does not declare the felt sense meaningless or ahistorical.

# Current research consequences

1. **Minimal internal conflict rules are preferable to a baroque inner constitution unless evidence shows added value.**
2. **No-arrears, helper concentration and provenance are useful because they sharpen implementation, not because they are novel mechanisms.**
3. **Depth escalation should be state-dependent rather than calendar-dependent.**
4. **The protocol should explicitly preserve alternative explanations and user contestability, especially around `protector`, `memory`, `authentic self`, and helper authority.**
5. **Known prior art should be promoted freely into the practical ledger when it improves runtime safety.**
