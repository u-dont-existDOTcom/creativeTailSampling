# Evidence ledger supplement — Batch 006 routing additions

Date: 2026-08-17  
Scope: evidence supporting conservative routing safeguards. Reddit cases are stress-test inputs, **not evidence that their claims or implied diagnoses are true**.

## Evidence warning

The new outer maps are architectural safeguards assembled from known clinical, ethical, and safety principles. They have not been validated as a unified routing system. Their benefit must be tested against simpler routing, false-positive escalation, missed emergencies, user burden, cultural variation, and real multi-turn outcomes.
