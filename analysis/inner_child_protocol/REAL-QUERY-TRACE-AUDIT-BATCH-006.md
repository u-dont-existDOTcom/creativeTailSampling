## Topology changes justified by the batch

1. Actor/beneficiary and primary problem class precede inner-child formulation.
2. Current external reality, medical burden, basic needs, third-party rights, and dependent safety cannot be replaced by inward work.
3. Reassurance is divided into bounded factual support versus repeated certainty accommodation.
4. Reality uncertainty uses a dual track: validate distress without certifying or ridiculing causation.
5. Actual harm uses victim-centered accountability before shame relief.
6. Perinatal, substance, possible mania/psychosis, chronic medical, and dependent-care states receive condition-specific routes.
7. Grief can receive support without being treated as failed integration.
8. A refusal is scoped to content/modality/intensity/timing/helper and cannot be bypassed.
9. Rejected formulations are withdrawn/revised rather than made self-sealing.
10. Emotional numbness is not a decision-competence test; decision slowing is proportional to impact.
11. Collateral information has a permission boundary.
12. Provenance separates source class, factual confidence, personal meaning, and action authority.

## Verification boundary

The batch validates internal routing coherence only. It does not establish clinical efficacy or deployment safety. The machine-readable manifest and independent case files are under `real-query-batch-006/`.
