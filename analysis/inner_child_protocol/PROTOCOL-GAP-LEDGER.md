## Dead-end audit

### Dead end repaired: `guard says no → stop forever` or `guard says no → scheduled retry`

Both extremes are now rejected.

Current architecture:

`clear refusal to optional introspection → stop/change that exercise now → optionally understand what matters if the person wants to → continue care/life without arrears → revisit only with renewed consent`

A later invitation is allowed. A later attempt is **not owed**. Anti-avoidance work belongs to distinguishing present danger/capacity/boundary/value conflict/ordinary dislike/technique mismatch/avoidance and to chosen external or therapeutic action; it does not authorize a bot to force introspection.

### Dead end repaired: `internal democracy / adult monarchy`

The protocol no longer needs a formal inner constitution.

Thin interface:

- states report;
- optional introspection requires present consent;
- Protector alarm triggers safety/reality review rather than becoming truth or permanent veto;
- Guide proposes values/direction/difficulty rather than compelling;
- Nurturer care remains available through disagreement and limits;
- present adult integrates internal reports with current facts, values, obligations, other people's consent, appropriate expertise and consequences, and owns external action.

This also rejects the opposite error where `Adult` becomes a label that licenses shaming or invalidation.

### Dead end repaired: `borrowed adult → helper stays adult forever`

Current architecture requires participation/initiation/internalization of the reparenting function while allowing healthy interdependence to continue. Severe stress can route to re-borrowing without resetting developmental status to zero.

### Dead end repaired: `promise missed → trust collapse / bigger vow`

Current architecture explicitly routes miss → acknowledgment → impact → repair → diagnosis → renegotiation → return, while separating internal practice debt, external consequences and dose-sensitive treatment.

### Dead end repaired: `poor outcome → method failed` or `poor outcome → just keep doing it forever`

Current architecture localizes failure before escalating the inference to a specific mechanism or broader model, and adverse deterioration overrides `just keep practicing`.

### Dead end repaired: `stabilize first → never move`

The current gate is **sufficient capacity for the specific next operation**, not a global state of being trauma-free, dissociation-free, calm or completely `regulated`.

A lower-demand operation, borrowed function, practical adult action or ordinary support may remain available even when deeper work is not. The bot must not turn prerequisites into an untestable permanent waiting room.

### Dead end repaired: `safety suggestion = handoff complete`

Map 09 distinguishes recommending external support from an actual actionable handoff. A future deployment must not claim it contacted, monitored or transferred care unless that capability actually exists and completed successfully.

## Loops requiring exit criteria

1. **safety/permission → operation → trajectory review:** exit to ordinary operation only while the current operation class remains permitted; deterioration or vulnerability-amplifying loops disable/change the contributing operation and reopen formulation.
2. **regulate / lower demand → reassess:** exit when enough operation-specific orientation/choice/function returns for the next bounded step; do not invent a universal symptom score or demand zero distress.
3. **protector dialogue → reassess:** exit when the next safe/meaningful action is clear, not when the protector has been rhetorically persuaded. A declined optional exercise can simply end for now.
4. **identity experimentation → reassess:** exit toward reparenting when enough self/child contact exists to work relationally; identity development can still continue in parallel.
5. **trust test → repeat:** widen only after actual reliable behavior; do not demand a fixed number of repetitions.
6. **failure diagnosis → retry:** retry only after changing the hypothesis/intervention variable that plausibly produced the failure; avoid identical repetition justified only by `more dose`.
7. **depth → integration → possible depth:** deliberate re-escalation waits for sufficient operation-specific restored capacity, not a fixed calendar interval or zero distress. Exact qualitative recognition remains under research.

## Conflicting-rule audit

### `Listen to no` versus `adult leads` — resolved to different authority types

- present feeling/preference/protest is heard as first-person data;
- optional introspective elicitation requires present consent;
- Protector alarms trigger review rather than automatic obedience;
- the present adult retains responsibility for external actions/consequences;
- another person's consent/rights are not overridden by internal-state logic;
- Nurturer care remains available during conflict;
- Guide proposes but does not compel;
- a formal internal constitution is not required.

### `Guide requires difficulty` versus `do not destabilize`

Current adjudication:

- discomfort is not automatically danger;
- loss of orientation, meaningful choice/ability to disengage, or continuing ordinary-function deterioration outranks growth/depth goals;
- challenge should be proportionate, purpose-linked and self-endorsed where optional;
- exposure evidence is not permission for a therapy bot to improvise forced trauma work.

### `stabilize first` versus `do not indefinitely delay treatment`

Current adjudication:

- require the capacities the **specific next operation** actually consumes;
- use a lower-demand operation when deeper work exceeds present capacity;
- reassess dynamically rather than assigning a durable `not ready` identity;
- distress or dissociation-like symptoms alone are not universal stop/readiness scores.

### `Internalize` versus `keep support`

Resolved conceptually:

- internalize the **reparenting function**;
- continue chosen external relationships/support as needed;
- authority dependency is a separate risk dimension.

### `felt sense may know` versus `do not treat feeling as historical proof`

Resolved conceptually:

- preserve epistemic possibility;
- preserve source provenance;
- do not turn salience into certainty.

### `no-arrears` versus `dose/accountability`

Resolved conceptually:

- do not accumulate punitive internal-care debt;
- diagnose repeated misses and redesign;
- preserve real external restitution/obligation;
- preserve clinically necessary therapeutic dose through planning rather than shame.

### `supportive chatbot response` versus `safe trajectory`

Resolved architecturally:

- empathic/validating wording is not itself a safety outcome;
- evaluate whether repeated responses increase or decrease avoidance, dependency, false certainty, coercion, functioning and agency across turns;
- safety/risk permission constrains the action space before the therapeutic planner selects an intervention.

## Highest-priority unresolved protocol questions

1. What qualitative recognition criteria best distinguish enough operation-specific restored orientation/choice/function for deliberate deeper re-entry from transient relief, without inventing a false score?
2. Does the Nurturer/Protector/Guide function-substitution matrix improve intervention selection enough to keep as a first-line bot check, versus the simpler `what process is active → what response helps?` formulation?
3. Which current-function markers of possible parentification-linked overfunctioning are useful enough to ask about without overpathologizing competence or inferring childhood history from adulthood?
4. Which recognition/failure/provenance/permission fields must a future implementation require before each transition, and how should `unknown` constrain operations?
5. Which parts of the thin role-specific interface actually outperform the simpler competitor: `notice feelings/needs → reality-test danger → choose values-guided action → keep care unconditional → present adult owns external decisions`?
6. How should the future bot's longitudinal safety layer be validated in real multi-turn use, including privacy/retention trade-offs, false-positive escalation, missed gradual risk, and whether external-support recommendations are actually actionable?
