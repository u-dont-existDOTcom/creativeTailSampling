## Dead-end audit

### Dead end repaired: `guard says no → stop forever` or `guard says no → scheduled retry`

Both extremes are now rejected.

Current architecture:

`clear refusal to optional introspection → stop/change that exercise now → optionally understand what matters if the person wants to → continue care/life without arrears → revisit only with renewed consent`

A later invitation is allowed. A later attempt is **not owed**. Anti-avoidance work belongs to distinguishing present danger/capacity/boundary/value conflict/ordinary dislike/technique mismatch/avoidance and to chosen external or therapeutic action; it does not authorize a bot to force introspection.

### Dead end repaired: `internal democracy / adult monarchy`

The protocol no longer needs a formal inner constitution.

Thin interface:

- states report;
- optional introspection requires present consent;
- Protector alarm triggers safety/reality review rather than becoming truth or permanent veto;
- Guide proposes values/direction/difficulty rather than compelling;
- Nurturer care remains available through disagreement and limits;
- present adult integrates internal reports with current facts, values, obligations, other people's consent, appropriate expertise and consequences, and owns external action.

This also rejects the opposite error where `Adult` becomes a label that licenses shaming or invalidation.

### Dead end repaired: `borrowed adult → helper stays adult forever`

Current architecture requires participation/initiation/internalization of the reparenting function while allowing healthy interdependence to continue. Severe stress can route to re-borrowing without resetting developmental status to zero.

### Dead end repaired: `promise missed → trust collapse / bigger vow`

Current architecture explicitly routes miss → acknowledgment → impact → repair → diagnosis → renegotiation → return, while separating internal practice debt, external consequences and dose-sensitive treatment.

### Dead end repaired: `poor outcome → method failed` or `poor outcome → just keep doing it forever`

Current architecture localizes failure before escalating the inference to a specific mechanism or broader model, and adverse deterioration overrides `just keep practicing`.

## Loops requiring exit criteria

1. **regulate → reassess:** exit when enough present orientation/choice/function returns for the next bounded step; do not invent a universal symptom score.
2. **protector dialogue → reassess:** exit when the next safe/meaningful action is clear, not when the protector has been rhetorically persuaded. A declined optional exercise can simply end for now.
3. **identity experimentation → reassess:** exit toward reparenting when enough self/child contact exists to work relationally; identity development can still continue in parallel.
4. **trust test → repeat:** widen only after actual reliable behavior; do not demand a fixed number of repetitions.
5. **failure diagnosis → retry:** retry only after changing the hypothesis/intervention variable that plausibly produced the failure; avoid identical repetition justified only by `more dose`.
6. **depth → integration → possible depth:** deliberate re-escalation waits for sufficient restored ordinary capacity, not a fixed calendar interval or zero distress. Exact qualitative recognition remains under research.

## Conflicting-rule audit

### `Listen to no` versus `adult leads` — resolved to different authority types

- present feeling/preference/protest is heard as first-person data;
- optional introspective elicitation requires present consent;
- Protector alarms trigger review rather than automatic obedience;
- the present adult retains responsibility for external actions/consequences;
- another person's consent/rights are not overridden by internal-state logic;
- Nurturer care remains available during conflict;
- Guide proposes but does not compel;
- a formal internal constitution is not required.

### `Guide requires difficulty` versus `do not destabilize`

Current adjudication:

- discomfort is not automatically danger;
- loss of orientation, meaningful choice, or ordinary function outranks growth/depth goals;
- challenge should be proportionate, purpose-linked and self-endorsed where optional;
- exposure evidence is not permission for a therapy bot to improvise forced trauma work.

### `Internalize` versus `keep support`

Resolved conceptually:

- internalize the **reparenting function**;
- continue chosen external relationships/support as needed;
- authority dependency is a separate risk dimension.

### `felt sense may know` versus `do not treat feeling as historical proof`

Resolved conceptually:

- preserve epistemic possibility;
- preserve source provenance;
- do not turn salience into certainty.

### `no-arrears` versus `dose/accountability`

Resolved conceptually:

- do not accumulate punitive internal-care debt;
- diagnose repeated misses and redesign;
- preserve real external restitution/obligation;
- preserve clinically necessary therapeutic dose through planning rather than shame.

## Highest-priority unresolved protocol questions

1. What qualitative recognition criteria best distinguish enough restored orientation/choice/function for deliberate deeper re-entry from transient relief, without inventing a false score?
2. Does the function-substitution matrix (wrong Nurturer/Protector/Guide response) improve intervention selection enough to keep as a first-line bot check?
3. What practical criteria best distinguish parentified survival-driven overfunctioning from flexible integrated adult capacity without pathologizing real competence?
4. Which recognition/failure fields are essential enough that a future bot must collect them before routing rather than infer them?
5. Which parts of the thin role-specific interface actually outperform the simpler competitor: `notice feelings/needs → reality-test danger → choose values-guided action → keep care unconditional → present adult owns external decisions`?
