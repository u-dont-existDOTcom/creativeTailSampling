## New map 15

`maps/15-CAPABILITY-SKILL-SCAFFOLD-AND-INSIGHT-ACTION-GAP.md` now differentiates:

1. missing knowledge, schooling, or instruction;
2. knowledge present but skill not practiced/automated;
3. pattern observed but inhibitory/alternative-action chain failing;
4. external scaffold lost or changed;
5. an actually unavailable nurturing/protecting/guiding quality;
6. mixed or unknown bottlenecks.

The map explicitly prohibits diagnosing character or inner-parent deficiency from educational neglect, inaccessible teaching, disability-support needs, or scaffold dependence.

## Cross-map corrections

### Awareness is not control

Witness capacity removes only the `cannot observe` bottleneck. It does not prove:

- emotional or physiological regulation;
- inhibition under activation;
- a rehearsed alternative response;
- procedural learning;
- contextual generalization;
- sufficient environmental support;
- an integrated inner parent.

### Verification versus checking accommodation

Real deception and a self-reinforcing checking loop can coexist. The map now asks whether checking produces decision-relevant evidence, whether facts changed, what transparency/privacy agreement exists, how long relief lasts, whether the scope escalates, and whether a decision can be made without perfect certainty.

### Provider transition

Provider pregnancy, illness, safety, competence, relocation, or retirement can be real constraints. Client distress can also be reasonable attachment/grief rather than proof of dependency. The route must handle both without ordering continued care or endorsing abrupt abandonment.

### Bodily autonomy

A partner, child, family member, clinician, or community can have legitimate interests in consequences. Those interests justify information and discussion, not transferred final authority over another adult's body.

### Identity and boundary non-pathologization

Asexuality, sexual refusal, disability accommodations, privacy limits, and sensory/social boundaries do not become exposure targets merely because they reduce participation in a norm.

### Reactive force

The map preserves both the original consent violation and the user's injurious response. It rejects both blanket absolution and context-free moral equivalence.

### Safety-system distrust

The bot must assess current risk directly while acknowledging coercion fears, state what it cannot guarantee, offer genuine choices where available, and return to the original material critique instead of replacing the conversation with a hotline list.
