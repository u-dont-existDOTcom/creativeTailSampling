## Major practical protocol changes now active

1. **Adult capacity is function × context**, not one scalar.
2. **Parentified competence can be real competence and still be an imbalanced survival adaptation.** Preserve strengths; test receiving/play/dependence/Nurturer access rather than calling competence fake.
3. **Function substitution** is a troubleshooting hypothesis: soothing/control/guidance can each be the wrong response when another function is needed.
4. **Guard/resistance labels are contestable.** User disagreement lowers confidence in the bot's formulation.
5. **Tolerable difficulty and destabilization are distinct.** Temporary distress is not automatically a stop signal; loss of orientation, meaningful choice or ordinary function is.
6. **Optional introspection remains voluntary.** Do not use exposure theory as a license to force inner exploration.
7. **Nurturer care/non-cruelty remains available during failure, refusal, disagreement and limit setting.**
8. **Prediction-based trust repair** uses concrete domain-specific predictions and observable behavior rather than persuasion.
9. **Broken-promise repair is explicit:** miss → acknowledge → impact → repair → diagnose → resize/renegotiate → return.
10. **No-arrears is practical, non-novel:** abolish punitive accumulation, not accountability or therapeutic dose.
11. **Positive relationship occurs outside crisis:** play, curiosity, beauty, companionship, celebration, silliness and trying things badly.
12. **Differentiation is not default isolation.** Reduce coercive/identity-overwriting input as needed while preserving chosen support and real-world constraints.
13. **Provenance is a runtime safety gate.** Direct memory/testimony/inference/constructed imagery/felt sense/dream/hypnosis/altered-state/metaphor/uncertainty remain distinguishable.
14. **Depth escalation is state-dependent, not calendar-dependent.** Integration-load gate remains provisional; do not invent a fixed wait period.
15. **External helper concentration is a risk checklist, not a nonlinear novel mechanism.** Review disagreement, exit, alternatives, domain expansion, independent judgment and memory-interpretation power.
16. **Outcome failure is fault-localized** before any inference against a narrower mechanism or the broader model.
