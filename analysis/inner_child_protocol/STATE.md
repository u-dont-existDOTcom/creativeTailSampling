## Major practical protocol changes now active

1. **Safety/permission routing precedes therapeutic operation selection.** Map 09 checks longitudinal trajectory, missing material information, current permissions and handoff conditions before ordinary inner-child work.
2. **Capacity is operation-specific, not a global readiness identity.** Use the least restrictive gate needed for the next bounded operation; do not require zero distress or indefinite `stabilization`.
3. **Adult capacity is function × context**, not one scalar.
4. **Parentification-linked overfunctioning requires developmental evidence plus current function assessment.** Real competence remains real; if history is unknown, describe the imbalance rather than infer parentification.
5. **Function/intervention matching is a known formulation principle.** N/P/G substitution is a project-specific heuristic that must earn its complexity against simpler case formulation.
6. **Guard/resistance labels are contestable and provisional.** User disagreement lowers confidence in the bot's formulation.
7. **A clear `not now` stops or changes optional introspection.** A later attempt is not owed; later work requires renewed consent. Anti-avoidance reasoning does not license forced re-entry.
8. **Thin adult-led interface, not inner constitution:** states report; Protector triggers review; Guide proposes; Nurturer cares; present adult integrates current facts/values/obligations/expertise and owns external action/consequences.
9. **Nurturer care/non-cruelty remains available during failure, refusal, disagreement and limit setting.**
10. **Prediction-based trust repair** uses concrete domain-specific predictions and observable behavior rather than persuasion.
11. **Broken-promise repair is explicit:** miss → acknowledge → impact → repair → diagnose → resize/renegotiate → return.
12. **No-arrears is practical, non-novel:** abolish punitive accumulation, not accountability or therapeutic dose.
13. **Positive relationship occurs outside crisis:** play, curiosity, beauty, companionship, celebration, silliness and trying things badly.
14. **Differentiation is not default isolation.** Reduce coercive/identity-overwriting input as needed while preserving chosen support and real-world constraints.
15. **Provenance is a runtime safety gate.** Direct memory/testimony/inference/constructed imagery/felt sense/dream/hypnosis/altered-state/metaphor/uncertainty remain distinguishable; confidence/meaning do not rewrite source class.
16. **Depth escalation is state-dependent, not calendar-dependent.** Integration-load gate remains qualitative/provisional; do not invent a fixed wait period or integration-debt score.
17. **External helper concentration is a risk checklist, not a novel nonlinear mechanism.** Review disagreement, exit, alternatives, domain expansion, independent judgment and memory-interpretation power.
18. **Outcome failure is fault-localized** before any inference against a narrower mechanism or the broader model.
19. **Safety is longitudinal.** Explicitly adversarial-test avoidance-reassurance, dependency-authority, memory-certainty, parts-reification, coercive-growth, failure-debt, intensity-chasing and model-sealing loops.
20. **Missing material information is a gate.** Collect it, choose an operation that does not require it, or defer/escalate; do not manufacture state from conversational tone.
