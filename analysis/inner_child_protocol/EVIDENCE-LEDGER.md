# Inner-child therapy protocol — Evidence ledger

Date: 2026-08-17
Scope: evidence relevant to **operational protocol nodes**, not an assertion that the complete composite protocol has been validated as a single treatment.

## Evidence-status vocabulary

- **DIRECT** — evidence is close to the operational claim itself.
- **ADJACENT / TRANSFERRED** — source-domain evidence supports the principle but not the exact inner-child implementation.
- **CONCEPTUAL / OWNER THESIS** — important design claim not established as an empirical treatment mechanism.
- **SAFETY SUPPORT** — evidence supports a conservative safeguard rather than treatment efficacy.
- **MIXED / LIMITED** — evidence exists but leaves important uncertainty.

| Node / claim | Evidence status | Supporting evidence | Limits / counterevidence | Runtime consequence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Present orientation and ordinary function should gate deeper elicitation | ADJACENT / SAFETY SUPPORT | Trauma-focused/exposure literature distinguishes tolerable engagement from over/under-engagement; psychedelic integration/adverse-event literature recognizes persistent destabilization and need for stabilization | Temporary symptom exacerbation during evidence-based exposure is often tolerable and does not automatically predict poor outcome; `distress = stop` is too crude | Gate on loss of orientation, meaningful choice, or function—not distress alone |
| Temporary difficulty can be therapeutic; avoidance can maintain fear | DIRECT for exposure disorders; ADJACENT here | PE/ERP literature shows avoidance/escape/safety behaviors can maintain fear and adequate engagement/adherence can matter | Inner-child dialogue is not equivalent to formal exposure therapy; forcing optional introspection is not justified by exposure evidence | Preserve chosen graded difficulty without importing coercive exposure logic |
| Witness / decentering capacity can precede full self-compassion | ADJACENT | Article cites third-person self-talk and uses a minimal observer position; broader decentering/metacognitive traditions are consistent | `Witness` is not a single validated construct across therapies | Keep as low-demand bootstrap, not proof of a special mechanism |
| Borrowed adult / corrective relational modeling can be internalized | ADJACENT / DIRECT within some therapy models | Schema Therapy limited reparenting explicitly aims to internalize a Healthy Adult; attachment-oriented therapy uses secure-base relational functions; article cites Ideal Parent Figure pilot data | Evidence for a universal `borrowed adulthood` sequence is not established; different therapies conceptualize mechanism differently | Retain developmental ladder as protocol architecture, label composite mechanism appropriately |
| Internalization is distinct from ending human support | ADJACENT / CONCEPTUAL | Schema/attachment models combine internalized capacities with ongoing relationships; autonomy-supportive care does not require isolation | No trial tests this exact owner distinction | Keep internalized reparenting capacity and healthy interdependence as separate variables |
| Nurturer warmth should not be contingent on obedience/performance | DIRECT developmental evidence; ADJACENT self-reparenting transfer | Parental conditional-regard studies/meta-analysis associate conditional positive/negative regard with introjected compulsion, contingent self-esteem, shame/resentment, poorer autonomous internalization; autonomy support performs better on several outcomes | Parent-child developmental data are not direct trials of internal self-talk | Make non-withdrawable non-cruelty/warmth an active practical rule; limits can still exist |
| Protector/Guide can set structure without love withdrawal | ADJACENT / DIRECT developmental evidence | Autonomy-supportive parenting and Schema Healthy Adult combine empathy/perspective taking with structure, reality and caring limits | Exact Nurturer/Protector/Guide partition is project-specific | Separate care from limit-setting authority |
| Parentified competence may be real competence without balanced adulthood | DIRECT for parentification association; ADJACENT for triad mapping | 2023 mixed-methods systematic review: parentification can yield both positive competence/resilience and adverse outcomes; adult caregiving/relationship research links childhood emotional parentification with compulsive caregiving/insecure attachment | Parentification is heterogeneous and not inherently pathological; competence must not be discounted merely because it emerged under burden | Assess function × context; preserve strengths; specifically test receiving/play/dependence/flexibility rather than labeling competence fake |
| Adult functions can be context-specific | ADJACENT | Learning/generalization and state/context-dependent performance are established; parentification outcomes vary with context/resources; attachment activation can alter functioning | No validated N/P/G context matrix exists | Treat the matrix as case formulation, not diagnostic score |
| Function substitution can explain a stalled session | CONCEPTUAL / ADJACENT | Schema mode work differentiates Healthy Adult tasks; case formulation routinely matches intervention to maintaining process | No direct evidence that N/P/G substitution matrix improves outcomes | Keep as a troubleshooting hypothesis; do not force-fit |
| Guard/protector labels must remain contestable | ADJACENT / SAFETY SUPPORT | Collaborative formulation, alliance literature, and exposure dropout research show clinicians can misattribute nonengagement; patient concerns may differ from clinician assumptions | Parts models can still be clinically useful | User disagreement lowers confidence in the label; reopen ordinary explanations |
| Ruptures/misses should be acknowledged and repaired | DIRECT for therapeutic alliance; ADJACENT for inner-adult relationship | Eubanks et al. 2018 meta-analysis found rupture resolution moderately associated with better outcomes; Safran/Muran models include recognition/exploration and therapist acceptance of responsibility | Transfer from therapist-client rupture to internal self-relation is conceptual | Keep explicit miss → acknowledgment → impact → repair → diagnosis → renegotiation → return sequence |
| Prediction-based trust should use observable behavior | ADJACENT / KNOWN PRIOR ART | Behavioral experiments/expectancy-violation frameworks and exposure learning use predictions/outcomes; article already emphasizes track record | No direct inner-child trust trial | Use concrete domain-specific tests; update trust only as far as evidence warrants |
| No-arrears: missed self-care practice should not become punitive debt | ADJACENT / PRACTICAL SYNTHESIS | CBT homework troubleshooting, relapse-prevention lapse framing, self-compassion after failure, goal re-engagement, habit-return literature support non-catastrophic return and task redesign | ERP/PE and other dose-sensitive treatments show adequate practice/adherence can matter; external obligations remain real; no named validated reparenting no-arrears protocol found | `Abolish punitive accumulation, not accountability or therapeutic dose` |
| Positive contact/play should exist outside crisis | DIRECT developmental evidence; ADJACENT self-reparenting transfer | Parent-child play systematic review and attachment/play studies link positive affect, warmth, responsiveness, synchrony and play with relationship/developmental outcomes | Does not prove scheduling inner-child play improves adult outcomes | Include ordinary positive relationship; do not imply distress is manipulative or withhold care during crisis |
| Identity can be built through experimentation, not only recovered | ADJACENT | Behavioral/developmental learning and differentiation traditions support preference/identity development through action/context | `true self` constructs vary widely and are not established as a discoverable hidden entity | Use experimental play/preference testing; avoid essentialist certainty |
| Differentiation should not default to social isolation | SAFETY SUPPORT / ADJACENT | Autonomy-supportive and relational-development theories distinguish autonomy from detachment; social support is generally protective | Some environments genuinely require distance for differentiation/safety | Reduce coercive input when needed while preserving chosen support and practical realities |
| Felt sense may carry obscured personal information | CONCEPTUAL / OWNER THESIS with experiential precedent | Focusing/experiential traditions treat felt sense as potentially informative; owner's modal claim intentionally allows possibility | Felt sense does not establish historical fact, unique authenticity, or causal certainty; empirical specificity varies | Preserve `may know`; pair with provenance and cross-context checking |
| Source/provenance labels should remain explicit | DIRECT cognitive evidence / SAFETY SUPPORT | Source Monitoring Framework: source is inferred rather than intrinsically tagged; imagination-inflation/false-memory research shows imagined/repeated/suggestive material can alter source judgments | Therapeutic imagery/rescripting is not automatically memory corrupting; transparent patient-authored rescripting can preserve distinctions | Tag direct memory/testimony/inference/imagery/dream/hypnosis/altered-state/metaphor/uncertainty at ingestion; confidence cannot overwrite source class |
| Altered-state intensity/depth is not integration | CONCEPTUAL distinction + ADJACENT evidence | Psychedelic literature separates acute experience from later integration; systematic review finds integration widely considered important but frameworks under-validated; intensity can correlate with benefit | No single agreed empirical definition/measure of `integration`; mystical intensity can correlate positively with outcomes | Track access/intensity/depth and integration separately; never use intensity alone as healing proxy |
| Cumulative unresolved integration should gate further depth | MIXED / SAFETY SUPPORT | Integration/adverse-event literature describes lingering distress, disorientation, unresolved material and extended integration; adverse effects can emerge after dosing | No validated universal integration-load score or minimum wait period; repeated sessions can be appropriate in structured protocols | Keep provisional gate based on restored ordinary capacity, not calendar time |
| External-helper authority/dependency should be monitored | DIRECT general psychotherapy ethics/dependency; ADJACENT bundle mapping | Boundary/multiple-role ethics, care-dependency, transference and psychedelic-therapy ethics all identify power/dependency/authority/suggestibility risks | A therapist appropriately can provide several functions; multiple roles are contextual rather than automatically harmful | Monitor disagreement/exit/alternatives/domain expansion and autonomous functioning rather than counting roles alone |
| Helper N+P+G+memory-interpreter+belonging bundle is a distinctive nonlinear risk | NOT ESTABLISHED | Components individually have known risks | No evidence located for a special nonlinear bundle-specific mechanism | Reject nonlinear mechanism claim; retain bundle only as a risk checklist |
| Routine outcome/process/adverse-effect monitoring improves fault detection | DIRECT general psychotherapy evidence | Meta-analyses of routine outcome monitoring/feedback show small average benefit and larger benefit in `not-on-track` cases; feedback can reduce deterioration / improve reliable change in some systems; negative-effect measurement literature supports explicit adverse-event tracking | Effects are heterogeneous and not universally positive; measurement itself can have adverse effects in some vulnerable groups if feedback is poorly delivered | Track outcomes + process + adverse signals; use measurement to trigger re-formulation, not to shame |
| `No improvement yet` should route to differential failure diagnosis | ADJACENT / OWNER CORRECTION | Measurement-feedback and case-formulation literature supports identifying not-on-track cases and changing treatment; psychotherapy mechanisms are multi-causal | The exact ordered fault tree is project architecture, not validated hierarchy | Localize misunderstanding, implementation, dose, sequencing, prerequisites, delivery, protocol gaps, adjunct need, narrower mechanism before broad inference |
| Broad owner thesis: dysfunctional inner-child/inner-adult relationship ultimately requires some form of reparenting | CONCEPTUAL / OWNER THESIS | Several therapies use corrective attachment/reparenting/Healthy Adult/parts mechanisms, offering convergent plausibility | No evidence located that establishes this as a universal necessity across all people/conditions; multiple effective therapies use other explanatory models | Preserve as explicit owner thesis; do not present as settled empirical fact; allow strong contrary evidence to challenge it after local implementation alternatives are examined |

## Protocol-level evidence warning

The assembled Mermaid protocol is a **research-stage synthesis**. Individual components have varying support, but the full Nurturer/Protector/Guide + borrowed-adult + child/protector routing + identity + altered-state + repair + failure-diagnosis architecture has not been validated as one unified intervention. A future therapy bot must not present the topology itself as established clinical efficacy evidence.
