## Article material that should **not automatically become a runtime node**

These sections may be valuable explanatory, autobiographical, philosophical, or motivational material without defining mandatory therapy-state transitions:

- `My Journey` and specific autobiographical substance/spiritual history;
- spiritual-bypassing argument as a broad cultural thesis;
- specific spiritual/metaphysical descriptions in `When Love Still Feels Missing`;
- the philosophical causal account in `How to Forgive Without Forgetting`;
- named teachers/resources;
- the appendix critique of IFS as institutional/framework comparison.

A future bot can discuss these when relevant, but the core state machine should not assume them as prerequisites or facts merely because they appear in the article.
