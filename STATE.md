Extend C013 cautiously.

> When seekers apply as couples/households or have linked placement constraints, can a federation matching process that is stable for individuals become unstable, infeasible, or systematically unfair once joint preferences are introduced?

- hospital/residency matching couples problem;
- complementarities in matching;
- household placement / co-location constraints;
- matching with contracts.

Target communal applications:
- couples wanting the same community;
- coparents/children needing linked placement;
- friendship/care dyads applying together;
- households willing to split only among nearby communities;
- communities with several openings and cohort-composition effects (C009).

- matching-with-couples mathematics is established;
