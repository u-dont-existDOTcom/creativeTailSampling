## Practical lessons generated by Batch 20

These should remain in `u-dont-existDOTcom/communities/COMMUNITY-DEVELOPMENT-LESSONS.md` even though novelty failed:

1. treat related daughter communities as lineage-correlated observations;
2. use sibling-daughter comparisons when informative, but explicitly model self-sorting at fission;
3. do not infer that every practice in a successful community package caused success—neutral/harmful practices can culturally hitchhike;
4. where safe, use natural or deliberate daughter variation/ablation to identify load-bearing practices;
5. reserve some cases from rule-design discussions to test whether constitutional rules generalize rather than overfit the disputes that created them;
6. prefer parsimonious rules unless complexity demonstrably improves unseen-case handling;
7. avoid standardizing away harmless institutional variation without a demonstrated gain;
8. pool rare failures/near misses across a federation;
9. do not evaluate safety using raw incident counts without accounting for reporting/ascertainment quality;
10. maintain a federation-level reliability/failure-memory library.
