# Creative Tail Sampling — Resume State

## Current seed

**F001: Modernity may have unnecessarily coupled occupational specialization with relational specialization.**

The active hypothesis is that many functions once carried through multiplex relationships were unbundled into separate specialist relationships and institutions. The next task is to discover consequences that do **not** collapse to familiar claims about social capital, loneliness, division of labor, network centrality, repeated games, or "community is good."

These are already below the project's novelty threshold.

## Exact next move

Run a fresh tail batch of at least 12 **plain propositions** derived from F001.

### Search directions worth probing

These are directions, not conclusions:

- whether multiplex ties alter the *dimensionality* of reputation rather than merely making reputation stronger;
- whether institutional specialization creates correlated failure because all needs become coupled to money/market access;
- whether multiplexity changes how accurately communities infer character from behavior;
- whether unbundling creates a hidden conversion problem: needs must first be translated into standardized categories before anyone is authorized to help;
- whether multiplexity changes the topology of conflict by giving adversaries unrelated channels through which cooperation must continue;
- whether the loss of multiplex ties causes a phase transition where informal reciprocity suddenly becomes impossible rather than declining smoothly;
- whether romantic overload is a special case of a broader 'residual multiplexer' phenomenon;
- whether people in unbundled societies become individually interchangeable inside institutions while simultaneously becoming socially irreplaceable to partners;
- whether markets and bureaucracies appear more efficient partly because relational coordination costs are externalized to households and intimate partners;
- whether restoring multiplexity requires changing *overlap of roles*, not increasing number of friends or frequency of contact.
