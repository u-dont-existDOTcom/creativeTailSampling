## Batches 43–48

### Batch 43 — disclosure-incentive / preference-falsification gate
No novelty survivor. Member self-report is an incentive-sensitive evidence channel; privacy, independent custody, leaver channels, concrete behavior items and mode-sensitivity tests are foundational.

### Batch 44 — embedded reporting-bias diagnostics
No novelty survivor. Prefer known-truth foils, randomized privacy/direct-vs-indirect comparisons, mirror vignettes and independent anchors over assumed ordinary variance. Combine diagnostics into a sample/community **reporting-pressure profile**, never an individual lie score.

### Batch 45 — linked household/couple matching
No novelty survivor. Matching-with-couples theory owns the instability result. C013 extension: model households jointly, disclose hard constraints early, admit exact stable matching may fail, and retain bounded placement slack/nearby-placement options where feasible.

### Batch 46 — confidential transfer-interest channel
No novelty survivor. A private federation transfer-exploration path can lower exit friction and provide a behavioral evidence channel, but transfer interest must be interpreted conditional on alternatives/search support and cannot replace welfare measurement.

### Batch 47 — cumulative small-N privacy
**C023 survived provisionally.** Intentional-community research already recognizes small-community anonymity risk, but repeated overlapping aggregates create a deeper composition/differencing problem. Maintain a release ledger, test reconstruction paths, use pooled/access-tiered publication, and manage formal privacy composition where relevant.

### Batch 48 — destination-blind fission partitioning
No novelty survivor because Hutterites already implement the target structure.

High-value lesson:
- build two viable groups first;
- protect family/care units;
- only then assign mother/daughter destination under a precommitted neutral process;
- compare functional capacity, not only asset book value;
- record transitional subsidy and post-fission outcomes.

Research extension: use `analysis/branching_process_covariates.md` to add partition mechanism, destination-blindness, skill/leadership balance, asset/function balance and support exposure to future C005 lineage studies.
